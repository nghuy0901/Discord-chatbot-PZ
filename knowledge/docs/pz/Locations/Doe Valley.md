# Doe Valley

Doe Valley is a large expanse of farmland in Project Zomboid , covering the majority of the north-central part of the map. The area of Doe Valley is almost entirely covered by farmlands and small farmhouses, and encompasses Fallas Lake . Rosewood borders the south, Riverside up north of the Valley, and the Doe Valley Forest to the west.

The player will most likely pass through this region while traveling between, from, or to these major townships.

## Overview

Doe Valley is the name of a massive stretch of land in Knox Country, with most of the area consisting of vast farmland with an odd house or a line of warehouses. However, two small villages in the Valley house most of the area's locations of interest: one unnamed hamlet in the western corner of the Valley and Fallas Lake to the north.

A few buildings may hold precious and large amounts of loot, such as the Jamieton Army Surplus . A handful of warehouses contain crates with huge amounts of vegetable and fruit sacks, although these crops are perishable and will rot within the first week. Regardless, this makes these warehouses a great source of compost for farming or food if you can get to them in time.

Due to the zombie population throughout the Valley being quite low, Doe Valley is generally a safe place, making any of the small groups of houses or solo houses located in the Valley a good place to settle in. Regardless, there are a few places within Doe Valley that have a decent zombie population, namely Fallas Lake and the village to the west of the valley.

### Fallas Lake

Fallas Lake is a named town in the northern district of Doe Valley. It has a lot of buildings and other locations of interest, which can be found in its article. North of Fallas Lake, Doe Valley's northernmost part consists of two smaller lakes and a number of small houses adjacent to wheat farms.

### West

To the western fringes of Doe Valley, there is an unnamed residential hamlet that is sometimes recognized by players as "Jamieton". This hamlet contains Lenny's Car Repair , a gas station , County Offices , and a small market with Food Market , butcher, bakery, medical clinic , clothing store , along with the hamlet's iconic Jamieton Army Surplus . While the army surplus contains the bulk of the worthwhile loot at the hamlet, it is also swamped by many zombies.

Surrounding "Jamieton" can also be found even more farmhouses and homesteads.

### East

The eastern district of Doe Valley is perhaps the less interesting. The only notable location is the Pony Roam-O, a small equestrian center, making this area bad for looting. However, the eastern district may make for a good rural base location, as the houses there are secluded, and two properties in this area have wells for infinite clean water, such as Hodgetts Farm .

### Central

The center of Doe Valley is one of the larger parts of the Valley. Covering a large amount of area south of Fallas Lake, and with Rosewood to the south, it is covered almost entirely by farmland. While being a massive chunk of space, it has surprisingly low amounts of anything notable, with the only buildings being lone houses and the odd produce warehouse.

## Strategy

- One of the safest regions in Knox Country, Doe Valley is a perfect location for those who seek peace and safety. Almost all of the Doe Valley is rural, and these rural areas have very low zombie density. There are a few spots that have the potential to be a good safehouse, with woods on the one side for log gathering, foraging, and open fields on the other side for farming. A couple of water sources, such as small lakes, ponds, and a river, can be found on the north and west of the Valley, which can be visited and camped for fishing. It is the place for a survivalist's heaven if you are into this rustic lifestyle.
- If players choose to make a base in Doe Valley, they must first ensure that they have brought everything they need from other towns. This means every basic necessity rarely spawns in Doe Valley for a character to survive or to live comfortably. Most notably, these do include skill books, vehicle parts, both ranged and melee weapons, spare winter clothing in winter, a generator with scrap electronics, canned food, and literature.
- Running out of gas or having the engine stop in the middle of nowhere is a serious danger that players may face. Unless they are lucky enough to be stranded near a village or a house with one car in the garage. Therefore, redundancy should be taken into mind before every road trip inside Doe Valley.
