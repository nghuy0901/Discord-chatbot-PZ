# Irvington

Irvington is a town in the southwest of Knox Country , directly south of Echo Creek . It is modeled after the real city of Irvington, Kentucky , with Kentucky 79 running through it. Just north of town is the Irvington Speedway , famous for various pre Knox Event racing events.

## Overview

Along with Echo Creek , Brandenburg and Ekron , Irvington is one of the towns added to the Knox Country map in build 42 . Irvington is set on Kentucky 79 , and is largely agricultural, with crop fields and animal pastures surrounding the town on nearly all sides, similar to Echo Creek and Ekron . However, the town is far larger than Echo Creek , and about one-third larger than Ekron . this can primarily be attributed to the large residential districts and overall low density yet moderately high number of structures, similar to Echo Creek .

Irvington is an overall medium difficulty town, being harder to survive in than Riverside or Echo Creek , but slightly easier, to about the same difficulty as Muldraugh . The zombie population, while not especially large compared to the town's size, is spread across a large area, which may create an illusion of a higher overall population. The overall population is fairly uniform, with pockets of higher population being in the southwestern outskirts and northern side of town, due to the presence of a shopping plaza and various public and industrial infrastructure, respectively.

Some key point of interest in Irvington are Well Regulated Militia Gear & Supply , the Irvington Gun Club , a Mass-Genfac Co. warehouse, the Irvington Fire Department , and the Irvington Speedway .

## Districts

Due to its rural nature, Irvington lacks defined districts.

Overall, many of the key points of interest are found on the southern outskirts, with some also being on the northern side of town. The two sides are separated by a large residential area, with the zombie population remaining fairly consistent throughout. The northern side of town is an exception to this however, particularly near the fire station and U-Store It . As a general rule, The population tends to increase the closer the player gets to the train tracks.

The southern outskirts also have a higher population that the rest of town, but not as prominently so as the north of town, with the exception of the shopping plaza to the southwest. This, combined with the fact that many of the key looting locations are present in this area, makes Irvington a compelling choice for supply runs.

## Strategy

- The greatest danger in Irvington is being swarmed by zombies from all directions, with no means of escape. This is also a danger in other towns, but due to the fairly uniform distribution of zombies, combined with the moderately large overall size of the town, it can be especially dangerous in Irvington. This is further compounded by how spread out the buildings are. Due to the larger distance between cover, zombies are more likely to spot the player when attempting to lose a horde. However, this also creates a large amount of open ground, which is good for dispatching groups of zombies and makes it more difficult for zeds to catch the player by surprise. Staying vigilant and aware of the player's surroundings at all times will make it nearly impossible for zombies to sneak up to them due to the large amounts of open ground they must traverse.
- It is not possible to spawn in Irvington without the use of mods. If traveling here from another town, always bring extra supplies for the trip.
- Irvington does not lack reliable spawns of good weapons like Echo Creek does. This makes dealing with the higher zombie population less of a problem.
- Fire should be used with caution in Irvington, especially in the northern side of town, as the key points of interest there are fairly close together. In general, however, there is a lesser risk of collateral damage than in other towns, as buildings are farther apart.
- It is recommended to have decent levels in combat skills if the player wishes to travel to this location. Barring that, most builds will be effective in Irvington.

## Trivia

- Irvington was first teased as an image of Irvington Speedway and Farming & Rural Supply , which then was confirmed on the flier .
- While Irvington, KY is a real-world location, the town, or at least parts of it, may also be inspired by the Texas town of Arlington, which serves as the main setting for the American television show King of the Hill. A recreation of the entire block from the show can be found adjacent to Irvington Elementary . It recreates the homes of Hank, Bill, Dale, Boomhauer, Kahn, Lucky, and Luanne homes, with many small nods to specific episodes of the show. Such references include Gnome in Hank and Peggy’s bedroom, the closet by the garage with Peggy’s small makeshift office, the hot tub at Boomhauer's house, and Dale's basement. The baseball field and the gun club could also be more subtle references to the show.
