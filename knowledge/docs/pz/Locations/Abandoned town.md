The abandoned town , nicknamed Tanglewood by the community based on graffiti found in one of the buildings, is an unnamed small, decrepit town between Brandenburg and Riverside .

## Overview

The town contains 26 homes, with only one seemingly still unkept, nicknamed the trapper's house . 23 of the homes are abandoned and are currently occupied by (undead) squatters and hobos, of these 1 is burnt down, and 1 is still under construction. Many of the abandoned homes have destroyed doors and windows, with trash, old or makeshift furniture, and graffiti inside. The abandoned homes have very minimal loot inside. As of unstable Build 42, there has been a substantial uptick in zombie spawns, it is currently unknown if this is a glitch or intended.

## Businesses

At the southern edge of the town, there is an abandoned saloon, a small checkpoint, and the Sunderland Hills Sanatorium , an abandoned sanatorium complex which contains 2 auxiliary apartment buildings. There is also a tunnel leading to a restricted brick building by the road. All locations have minimal loot. The town also hosts C.G.E. Corp factory. McCoy Logging Co. containers can be found near the house construction site with crates with logs inside.

## History

Tanglewood can track its history back to 1911 with the construction of the Sunderland Hills Sanatorium. In 1962, one year after the closure of the Sanatorium, the C.G.E. Corp factory opened its doors. Likely saving the town due to only a small store/gas station and bar being the only other business. This led to dozens of manufacturing and production jobs and a booming economy. Following this was this town's heyday from 1962 to 1980. After the closure of the factory for unknown reasons, the town deteriorated to the state the town is in now. Inhabited by homeless and squatters with one “true” lone resident.

## Trivia

- This location is often referred to as Tanglewood due to a piece of graffiti on one of the buildings. It should be noted, however, that it isn't the only place that has this graffiti painted.
- This region is considered a historic area, according to the fliers .
- There was an attempted restoration effort underway prior to the Knox infection, unfortunately little progress was made before the apocalypse.
- While difficult to discern, there are overgrown dirt trails in the center area.
