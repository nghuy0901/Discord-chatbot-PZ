West Point is a town in the state of Kentucky, in an area known as Knox Country , and based on the real-life West Point, KY . West Point is located on the banks of the Ohio River , north of Muldraugh , east of Riverside , and south of Valley Station and Louisville . Dixie Hwy (Route 31W) runs through the southern area of West Point and curves north.

## Overview

West Point is the easternmost spawn town, with Louisville only a short drive away. However, it is one of the most dangerous areas in all of Knox Country . This is due to a large population of zombies spread throughout the town, along with a relative scarcity of high-quality melee weapons in the suburbs. Spawning in West Point is not recommended for new players.

West Point is an overall hard difficulty town, and widely considered one of the hardest places to survive in all of Knox Country . Large amounts of zombies can be found practically everywhere . Managing and fighting the many zombies here may be too difficult for newer players.

However, West Point's dense zombie population also leads to it having more loot. This, along with West Point's many good looting locations, makes the city rich with loot, particularly firearms . There are several areas where they can reliably spawn, and ammo is often abundant, with a large amount of it coming from the houses in the residential area. It is normal to find multiple firearms within a single home.

Some key points of interest in West Point are the police station , the hardware store , the gun store , the factory , and the American Tire location.

## Districts

West Point can be divided into two districts:

### Suburbs

The larger, suburban side occupies the western side of town and is where most character spawns are located. The houses tend to be rather large and do commonly feature garages, which often contain many useful items and weapons . There is a lack of any particular points of interest as the vast majority of this district is composed of residential buildings. This can be problematic as this area is swarming with many zombies that can easily overwhelm the player if they are under-equipped or have low combat skills.

### Downtown

The downtown occupies the eastern side of town and is where almost all major looting locations in West Point are. Most of the points of particular interest are located close to each other, but there are also more zombies in this area than in the suburbs. Almost all key points of interest in West Point are located on the eastern side of town.

## Strategy

- Spawning in West Point is a considerable challenge due to the lack of skills most characters have at the start of the game. Players should be prepared as such if they choose to spawn here. If the player wishes to travel to West Point from another city, it is a good idea to have levels in combat and nimble skills. Characters who start in West Point may have a hard time in the beginning due to their low levels.
- While the suburbs do contain large amounts of weapons, they may require levels in combat skills or significant mechanical skill to use effectively. The standard high-quality weapons such as axes or garden forks can be somewhat scarce in the suburbs. A solution to this can be to utilize the large amounts of firearms found within the suburbs. However, overly relying on one or the other can be a bad idea, depending on which skills the character has.
- Characters that rely extensively on stealth will be at a severe disadvantage in West Point. There are too many zombies to effectively sneak around.
- West Point has the best overall loot out of all the four starting towns, offering almost everything for the taking. Notably, the city is also rife with firearms and ammunition, so it can be a good location for a guns-based character to start in.
- Fire bombs and Molotovs are highly effective against large groups of zombies. However, due to the density of buildings in West Point, major collateral damage is a very real possibility.
