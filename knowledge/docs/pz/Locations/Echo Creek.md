Echo Creek is a small town in Knox Country surrounded by farmlands and forests. It is situated east of Ekron and north of Irvington , and connected to them via Kentucky 60 .

## Overview

Echo Creek is the smallest and westernmost spawn town, and serves as a great location for the player to spawn in if they wish to explore the new towns added to the map in Build 42 . The town is divided by a creek that flows into Wadsworth Lake , hence the name. Many of its residents likely worked in the surrounding farmlands before the Knox Event .

Echo Creek is an overall easy-medium difficulty town, being easier to survive in than Muldraugh , but slightly harder to survive in than Rosewood or Riverside . The town offers balanced loot spawns and a low to medium zombie population. However, the zombie population here can be surprisingly large in some areas despite the town itself being rather small, and zombies are likely to continue to migrate into town once killed from several nearby high-population map cells. This, combined with a general tendency for the town to lack strong melee weapons , can make Echo Creek deceptively difficult to survive in at times, despite the initial zombie population being relatively low.

Some key points of interest in and around Echo Creek are Speedy GO , a Diner location that makes for a great base, Ovo Farms , and Guns Unlimited .

## Districts

Due to the town's extremely small size, it lacks defined districts. Most of the businesses listed in the section below are located outside the town itself, but in relatively close proximity to it.

Overall, Echo Creek is primarily composed of small, single-story houses and trailers, with some having basements. West of main street lies the remains of an outdoor festival, which is guaranteed to contain a generator. The outskirts of town are composed of various crop and animal farms. Overall, the zombie distribution is fairly uniform, with pockets of higher concentration around various points of interest on the outskirts and roughly in the middle of the town.

## Strategy

- The greatest danger in Echo Creek is simply getting unlucky with loot spawns. Due to the town lacking guaranteed spawns for high-quality melee weapons such as axes and crowbars , if the player does not find many melee weapons in houses, they may be under equipped as a result of pure bad luck. Despite this, due to the town's relatively low zombie population compared to other towns, large amounts of high quality weapons are typically not needed as much as in other towns, considering default loot and zombie populations. Most weapons will likely be sufficient for most players.
- The Diner , it's second floor living space, and attached grocery store and auto shop, make for great places to establish a base.
