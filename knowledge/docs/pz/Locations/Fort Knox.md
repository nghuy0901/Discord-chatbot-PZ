# Fort Knox

Locations Knox Country • Abandoned town • Brandenburg • Bright Valley Trailer Park • Coalfield • Dixie Mobile Park • Doe Valley • Echo Creek • Ekron • Exclusion Zone border • Fallas Lake • Irvington • Louisville • March Ridge • Muldraugh • Ohio River • Riverside • Rosewood • Scenic Grove Mobile Home Park • Valley Station • West Point

Fort Knox is a large fort or military base in the Knox Country , named after Henry Knox . While currently players cannot visit it in the game, there are multiple references to it in the lore and VHS .

Most of its background comes from the real-life Fort Knox , the founding date, its usage during the Civil War, and other historical facts and figures. The fort was mainly used for storage of the important national documents and gold reserves. During the Knox Event , it was blockaded along with the rest of the Knox Country and became a quarantine research zone where the military, under General John McGrew supervise, were holding people for tests.

The addition of the Fort to the rest of the game map is planned after the implementation of the NPC features, specifically after the inclusion of military personnel.

March Ridge seems to be a military housing complex for Fort Knox. Despite not having anything actually military there other than the underground bunker, March Ridge's cookie-cutter housing, single entrance guarded by security gate, and position in relation to the real world suggest that March Ridge is housing for Fort Knox.

## Trivia

- Fort was commonly used during World War 2 as a filming location of numerous television shows and movies like Operation Fort Knox .
- Fort Knox Documentary references most of the historical events that occurred at the Fort, most of them are just a direct reference to real-life events.
- The Fort Knox sign in the game files is copied from the real-life Fort Knox sign.
