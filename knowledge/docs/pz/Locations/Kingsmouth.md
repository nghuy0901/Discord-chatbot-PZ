# Kingsmouth

Challenges A Really CD DA • A Storm Is Coming • House In The Woods • Kingsmouth • Opening Hours • Studio • The Descending Fog • Winter Is Coming • You Have One Day

Kingsmouth is a challenge map that was added in Build 41 . It is a 'paradise island' vacation resort in the Caribbean, which has unfortunately succumbed to the Knox Event infection . The map features vast hotels, seaside locales, a military installation and various other points of interest. Xeonyx , the creator of the New Denver / Slocan Lake map is responsible for the creation of this map.

## In-game challenge description

Enjoy a relaxing stay on Kingsmouth island!

## Strategy

- Due to low amounts of loot in the starting spawn location, and the absolutely massive amount of zombies in the resort to the south, it is recommended that players leave the area and establish a base elsewhere.
- There is a small island at the southern tip of Kingsmouth accessible only through a single bridge. It contains a house with an antique oven and has very few zombie spawns, making it an ideal place for a small base.
