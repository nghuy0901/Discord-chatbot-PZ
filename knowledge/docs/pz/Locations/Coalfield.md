# Coalfield

Coalfield , also spelled Historical Coalfield , is a Wild West themed tourist attraction located in Doe Valley Forest , accessible via Kentucky 60 . Despite the lack of modern items, there is still some highly useful equipment to be found here.

## Overview

The mock town consists of just over a dozen mock homes and multiple Wild West themed mock businesses. To the west of the attraction is a hanging post platform and a large mock graveyard, and to the east is a large barn.

While most of the buildings contain little more than bottles and other trinkets and souvenirs, the blacksmith contains extremely useful items and crafting stations for blacksmithing , such as an advanced forge and various other tools and equipment.

The town also has various useful structures, such as rain collector barrels , animal troughs , and old stoves scattered throughout the area, making it a good source of these structures if the player lacks the skills or resources necessary to build them.

The zombie population in this area is rather low, making it a safe location to rest, establish a safehouse, or gather various crafting materials.

## Strategy

- The existence of valuable pre-built crafting stations, convenient road access, and close proximity to a gas station can make this area a very valuable base location. However, most towns are quite far from this location, making supply runs longer and practically impossible without a vehicle .
- There is a small stream to the west, allowing infinite collection of water and fishing , but high foraging , farming , or other skills that allow a renewable source of food are also a good idea.
