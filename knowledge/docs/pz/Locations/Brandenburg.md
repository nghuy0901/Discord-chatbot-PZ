Brandenburg is a town in northwest Knox Country , directly west of Riverside . It is modeled after the real city of Brandenburg, Kentucky . The town experienced a tornado on April 18, 1993, causing significant damage to the southeastern side of town.

## Overview

Brandenburg is one of the largest towns in Knox Country . However, this is primarily due to the lower building density on the western side of town. The town is situated on the banks of the Ohio River , making fishing very convenient. It also makes the town easy to reach from Riverside by simply following the coast westward. However, the high zombie population, especially near key points of interest, poses a significant challenge to players wishing to gather supplies here. Bright Valley Trailer Park is located east of town.

Brandenburg is an overall medium-hard difficulty town, being more difficult to survive in than Muldraugh and Irvington , but far easier than Louisville . This is primarily due to the town's size and mostly uniform zombie distribution, similarly to Irvington but to a more extreme degree. Brandenburg is estimated to contain approximately 5600 zombies on default settings, and is not to be underestimated. Zombies also tend to cluster in large numbers near most of the town's key points of interest, which makes supply runs a challenge.

In general, Brandenburg can be very closely compared to Irvington . Both towns are similarly sized, and have similarly uniform zombie distribution throughout the town. However, Brandenburg typically has approximately 50-100 more zombies per map cell on average, the outer, less dense suburbs are far more populated than Irvington 's are, and the presence of a commercial district means that zombies are typically clustered tighter around key looting areas, rather than truly uniform. There is also a large population of zombies near the Military blockade of the bridge to the northwest, and in the prison a short distance from said bridge. Despite all this, however, the increased zombie population also results in more loot, which can help offset the high number of zombies that are located here. Brandenburg is also quite far from most spawn towns, and as such the player is more likely to be adequately prepared before visiting.

Some key points of interest in Brandenburg are the P.S. Delilah , the Military blockade , Memento Ammori , Brandenburg Fire , and the pawn shop.

## Districts

### Residential

The large northern residential district makes up most of the town, and is composed of both trailers and houses of various sizes. Boyd Road runs through the middle of the district and connects it to the southern commercial district. Various businesses are found along this road, including a tool store, which can be a good place to find melee weapons . Zombie population is fairly uniform, with clusters of higher population surrounding the P.S. Delilah , most businesses along Boyd Rd, and particularly the prison and Military blockade to the northwest.

### Commercial

The southern commercial district is where most of the key points of interest are located. The total zombie population in this area is roughly the same as in the residential district, but they are packed far closer together, which may create the illusion of a higher overall population. Most zombies here are clustered around various stores and other areas rich with loot, rather than spread over a wider area like in the residential district.

## Strategy

- The greatest danger in Brandenburg is exhaustion from constant fighting, especially when taking the large zombie population into account. Be sure to rest adequately, and remember that it is not necessary to clear large swaths of the town in a single day. It is easy to get swarmed while doing so.
- It is not possible to spawn in Brandenburg without the use of mods. If traveling here from another town, always bring extra supplies for the trip.
- Brandenburg's eastern side is significantly more urban than Irvington , yet has approximately the same, to higher zombie population. This can be a challenge, as the comparative lack of wide open ground can make hordes more difficult to spot, increase the odds of surprise attacks, and increase the odds of unwanted close encounters. Due to this, it is recommended to not venture too far when fighting large groups of zombies, as it is possible to encounter even more zombies when attempting to kite or otherwise maneuver around zombies during combat. This can also make losing hordes more difficult as well. The player is equally likely to encounter a different group in or near the area they are using to evade the previous one.
- Firearm can be highly effective in Brandenburg, as there is an abundance of reliable ammo spawns when compared to most other towns.
- Having moderately high fitness , Nimble , and other combat skills is recommended. However, due to Brandenburg not being a spawn town, players are likely to have adequate skills by the time they have the supplies necessary to combat the high zombie population here.
