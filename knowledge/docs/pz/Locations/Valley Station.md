# Valley Station

Valley Station is a large area located to the south of Louisville . It is based on the real-life Valley Station, Louisville . Route 31W runs through the west section of Valley Station.

The Crossroads Mall can also be found in Valley Station.

## Overview

### Riverbank Valley Station

The westernmost section of Valley Station, this area is immediately along the banks of the Ohio River , and is centered along the Dixie Highway. As a result, this area is somewhat more developed than other parts of the region.

On its southern end, it has Drag Racing , Pole Position , and a Fossoil gas station. In the middle section of the area, there is a moderately large collection of small houses to the west of the highway. Towards the north, near the Exclusion Zone border , there is Ruby Gas , a church , school , and warehouse . Immediately south of the fence there also lies the Kentucky Rail trainyard.

### Inner Valley Station

This section of Valley Station lies deeper within the region and further away from the Dixie Highway. It is mostly comprised of spread out farms and residential buildings, and has almost no businesses. While there is not much there to be found, this area can make for a good place to live a laid-back life closer to nature and animals while still being in close proximity to Louisville to gather loot.

### Knob Creek

A small group of houses centered along Knob Creek Road, to the west of what is presumably, the Knob Creek. Based on the presence of a soup kitchen , it may be possible that this area is downtrodden, but there is nothing else to support that theory. Next to each other is The Straight Shooter bar and Guns-a-BlazInn motel.

The hallmark points of interest here are the Knob Creek Hunting Lodge and the adjacent shooting range , both excellent places for players to easily acquire firearms and ammunition. There is also a nearby Cafe .

### Crossroads Mall

This area is a massive commercial and retail complex, centered around a large StarEPlex cinema and the Crossroads Mall , which are both located on a gargantuan parking lot. Barring the cinema and the mall, there are also many other businesses in the area, including a moderate-sized strip mall and a Gas 2 Go station. However, players should know that the parking lot is absolutely swarming with zombies, but the wide open area does give players a combat advantage.

Directly east of the mall, there are what appears to be luxury housing developments that are still under construction. Northeast of the mall itself are a group of middle-income houses, along with some more under construction houses.

### Forest Valley Station

To the south of the Crossroads Mall is a massive forested region, and is part of the Jefferson Forest . The Knob Creek continues its course and flows parallel to Bearcamp Road and Salt River Road before merging with the Salt River . There is only one point of interest here, that being Camp Fitzgerald , a sprawling campgrounds located somewhat deep in the woods.
