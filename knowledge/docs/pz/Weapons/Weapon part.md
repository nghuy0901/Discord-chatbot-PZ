# Weapon part

Items Ammo • Animal part • Appearance • Camping • Clothing • Communication • Container • Cooking • Corpse • Electronics • Explosive • Fire source • Fishing • Fluid container • Food • Fuel • Gardening • Household • Instrument • Junk • Light source • Literature • Material • Medical • Memento • Security • Sports • Tool • Trap • Vehicle maintenance • Weapon • Weapon part • Debug

Weapon part , previously known as weapon mod , can be attached to certain firearms , modifying its stats.

## Usage

“ You'll need a screwdriver to mount/unmount them, they'll increase or decrease some stats of your weapon — Romain 'RJ' Dron

To attach a weapon part, a screwdriver , the appropriate weapon to modify and the weapon part, must all be in the player 's main inventory . Upon right-clicking the weapon, an option to "Upgrade" will appear, showing all currently available weapon upgrades. Upon left-clicking the desired upgrade, the player will equip both the screwdriver and the weapon part before attaching it to the weapon. To remove the weapon mod, simply follow the same steps, however choosing the option to "Remove Upgrade" followed by the desired upgrade to be removed.

## Location

“ Those modifications will come with some luck directly mounted on the rifle, or you can find separated parts at a gunstore. — Romain 'RJ' Dron

Weapon parts can be found either already attached to a weapon or inside one of the various gun stores located throughout Knox Country .
