A generator is a device used to provide electricity using gasoline .

## Overview

There are 4 types of generators: Lectro-Max (Red), Premium Technologies (Orange), ValuTech (Blue), and Old (Yellow, with a large circular section). Each type differs in sound, weight, and reliability. Regardless of type, each generator powers devices in a cylindrical area centered on itself with a radius of 20 tiles. The powered area reaches 3 floors above the generator and 3 floors below it.

## Usage

Generators must be placed on the ground, connected, fueled, and turned on, in order to provide power in its radius.

Generators can be picked up with the context menu, but do not go into a player's inventory. Instead they must be carried. This means the player cannot attack while carrying a generator, and if a weapon is selected, the generator will be placed on the tile the player is on. The player is also encumbered , meaning they move slower. All generators except the ValuTech variant have an encumbrance of 40, with the ValuTech having 30.

Generators can be heard by zombies , which will attract unwanted attention. Lectro-Max and Premium Technologies generators can be heard from a base radius of 20 tiles, with Old generators having an audible range of 25 tiles and ValuTech having a range of 23 tiles.

Players should not place a generator inside a structure they plan to, or are currently occupying. Generator exhaust will negatively impact character health very quickly down to 1. Leaving the structure will halt the damage, but the structure will not be safe until the generator is turned off.

Generators can be connected to the power system of a house via the "Connect Generator" option in the generator's context menu. A generator is fueled with gas via the "Add Fuel" context menu.
The tank of a generator can take up to 1 gas can 's fuel and can only be filled while the generator is off. There is no option to siphon fuel back out of a generator, but the fuel will stay in it if you pick it up and move it later.

Players need level 3 electrical , the electrician occupation, or to have read the How to Use Generators magazine to connect a generator.

If the Generator Working In Exterior Custom Sandbox world setting is enabled, generators may also be hooked up to other buildings around the world. One of the most practical uses for this is to power gas station pumps, allowing for continued access to the station's fuel reserves.

### Range

The range (radius) is 20 tiles on the x-y plane, as well as 3 floors above and below (forming a cylinder centered on the generator with a height of 5 and a base diameter of 41).

### Consumption

The baseline fuel consumption of a generator running with no load placed on it is 0.002 L/h. Any powered devices within a 20-tile radius add load to the generator, increasing fuel consumption. Various devices add different loads to the generator. The total load placed on a generator, along with electrical demand per object, can be seen by right-clicking the generator and selecting "Generator Info". The following table has each type of device, and its usage per tile in L/h. E.g., a popsicle freezer is 2 tiles and uses 0.016 L/h.

To calculate generator runtime, the following equation can be used:

amount of fuel remaining / power demand = runtime

- Number of liters of fuel remaining.
- Power demand in L/h.
- Runtime is measured as in-game hours.

Example: A popsicle fridge (0.016 L/h), television (0.003 L/h), 6 lamps (0.0012 L/h), and the base consumption (0.002 L/h) would equal a total fuel consumption of 0.0222 L/h, so a full generator of 10 liters would run for about 450 in-game hours (18.7 days).

### Repairing

Each generator variant has a different chance of durability loss, with the ValuTech variant having the highest chance to degrade. If the condition drops lower than or equal to 20%, there is a chance for the generator to catch on fire or explode.

## Obtaining

Generators are most often found in warehouses, garages, tool sheds, and self storage facilities.

There are also multiple places on the map where players can find guaranteed generators, like the military blockade near Louisville, Echo Creek festival zone, Doe Valley Secret Bunker and others.
