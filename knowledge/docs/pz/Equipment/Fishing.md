# Fishing

Player Moodle • Skill • Trait • Occupation • Inventory • Nutrition • Sleep • Condition • Health • Knox Infection

Fishing Fishing General Type Survivalist Modifies Time to catch fish Technical Skill ID Fishing

Fishing is a survivalist skill and an activity that allows characters to use a fishing rod combined with bait to catch various types of fish. Increasing the fishing level increases the quality of caught fish.

This ability is a great way to feed and fatten up a player living near a body of water , as fish often have high satiety and have many calories. Large lakes and rivers, especially the Ohio , are prime grounds for any prospective angler.

## Usage

To begin fishing, a fishing rod and some bait are required. Enter the combat stance next to a body of water and use the targeting cursor to cast your line. Cast your line at the ripples in the water to have the best chance of catching fish. To catch any fish, bait will be needed; otherwise, you will only catch trash.

Upon casting a line, you will see a meter appear above your character. This meter shows how much tension the fishing line has. If there is too much tension, the line may degrade or even snap. The fishing minigame only has two simple controls: ) for reeling in the line and ) to unreel the line and give it slack. You'll know if you have caught something if your character suddenly arches backwards and starts operating the fishing rod. Reel in the line to pull the fish closer to you.

Fish often will tug the line in their attempt to break free. In this case, you'll have to slacken the line to prevent it from snapping and reel the line again to continue pulling the fish once the line tension is reduced. This will result in fishing being a battle between you and the fish, but rest assured, this battle can be won with patience, and sooner rather than later, you'll have a filling and nutritious meal ready to eat.

Catching something by fishing will give Fishing XP. The heavier the fish that is caught, the more XP you will earn. A higher fishing skill will result in increasingly bigger, heavier, more filling, and fatty catches.

### Tips

- Fishing in dusk (18:00 - 21:00) and dawn (04:00 - 07:00) will also provide a buff towards catching fish.
- Standing at least seven tiles into the water, such as on a pier, will give a "deep water" buff.
- You can roughly gauge what has been caught on the hook based on the line's tension. Trash will have virtually no tension, while increasingly bigger fish will be stronger and fight more when you try to reel them in.
- It is highly recommended to keep the fishing site free of zombies.
- Reading the skill books will make the skill fast to level up, even if you started with zero in fishing.
- Living near or at a large body of water can help foster fishing habits.

## Repairs

Quite often, a rod will lose its line while fishing. Relining a rod requires an item from each of the following categories:

- Nail or paperclip
- Twine or fishing line

When used in the repair, a nail or paperclip is consumed, while the twine or fishing line consumes some of its contents (units).
A fishing rod doesn't lose durability when used for fishing.

### Fish

There are 3 types of sizes per fish: little, medium, and big. Each type of fish has a minimum size and a maximum size. A number is chosen at random between the minimum size and the maximum size to get the fish size. Each fish has a weight modifier; for example, trout has 60, meaning a trout of 30 cm / weight modifier (60) = 0.5 kg. Then the hunger reduction is based on the weight of the fish: weight * 10 (ex: 2 kg * 10 = 20 hunger reduction).

With live bait, the distribution of sizes is 50% small, 30% medium, and 20% big. This is 60% small, 25% medium, and 15% big for fishing tackle.

The various types of fish that can be caught range from common bass to large pike .
