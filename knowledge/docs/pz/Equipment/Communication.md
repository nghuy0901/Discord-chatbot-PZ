# Communication

Items Ammo • Animal part • Appearance • Camping • Clothing • Communication • Container • Cooking • Corpse • Electronics • Explosive • Fire source • Fishing • Fluid container • Food • Fuel • Gardening • Household • Instrument • Junk • Light source • Literature • Material • Medical • Memento • Security • Sports • Tool • Trap • Vehicle maintenance • Weapon • Weapon part • Debug

Communication items include radios , ham radios , walkie talkies , and televisions . These items can be used to watch or listen to in game communications, and communicate with other players in multiplayer .

Each tier of walkie talkie and ham radio have different frequencies and ranges they can operate on, while radios and televisions can all view/listen to the same channels regardless of the tier. Some televisions are unable to play VHS tapes.
