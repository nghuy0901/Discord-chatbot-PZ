# Fluid container

Items Ammo • Animal part • Appearance • Camping • Clothing • Communication • Container • Cooking • Corpse • Electronics • Explosive • Fire source • Fishing • Fluid container • Food • Fuel • Gardening • Household • Instrument • Junk • Light source • Literature • Material • Medical • Memento • Security • Sports • Tool • Trap • Vehicle maintenance • Weapon • Weapon part • Debug

A fluid container is any item or entity that can be used to store a fluid . Any fluid container can store any fluid.

## Overview

Fluid containers are essential for storing, transporting, and utilizing various fluids, such as water or gasoline . They can be used to mix compatible fluids, with properties such as nutrition and alcohol being combined and tracked. Additionally, they serve as vessels in crafting recipes and are useful for tasks like water purification and rain collection. Containers vary in capacity, encumbrance, the fluids they may spawn with, and functionality, such as rain collection and the ability to boil water.

Containers with water can be used with the game's autodrink feature regardless of their capacity. However, those with a capacity greater than 3L (3000mL) cannot be used for drinking via the context menu, and will display the tooltip:

Some fluid containers, such as gas tanks and water dispensers , can only be used for outputting fluid, so they cannot be refilled.
