# Quick tips

Quick tips are small notes that appear while a save is being loaded. They were added with Build 42.13.0 .

There are currently 53 regular tips and 27 marked as jokes, and they appear randomly.

## Tips

- You can shoot from inside a vehicle if your window is open.
- Keep accidentally Shouting? You can change the keybind in the Options menu.
- Accidentally closed your minimap? Hover over the map icon towards the upper left, then click the second map icon that pops out.
- Watch your rear! A zombie attack from behind means almost certain death.
- If you die, you can continue playing in the same world as a new character.
- Gain Strength and Fitness using the exercise options on the Health menu, or just run around a lot while carrying heavy stuff.
- VHS tapes can be used to catch up on TV shows you missed.
- You need a hose and an appropriate container to siphon gas from cars.
- Have a varied diet if you don't want to lose or gain too much weight.
- Set an alarm on your watch or clock to make sure you don't oversleep. Be warned - the sound can attract zombies.
- Sprinting tires you out, and will attract more zombies.
- Press C to crouch and move stealthily. Stick close to walls and your character will crouch down lower, becoming even harder to spot.
- Some knives can do a "jaw stab" that instantly kills zombies. But beware - your knife can also get stuck inside a zombie.
- The keys to a car can sometimes be found on the ground or in a house near it, or in the inventory of a nearby zombie.
- Turning on a generator indoors will make the fumes kill you.
- Wound infection (NOT Knox Virus infection) can be avoided by using clean bandages. Sterilize bandages using disinfectant or boiling water.
- Putting items in your belt or holster lets you access them the items quicker, and reduces their weight. Bug
- Rotten food in a composter will slowly turn into compost, which can be used to fertilize crops.
- The sound of vehicle horns or sirens will attract zombies from a large radius.
- Lost something on the ground? Use Search Mode on the Investigate Area menu to find it again.
- Your crops can be trampled by zombies, so make sure to grow them somewhere safe.
- Use notepads, journals, and similar items to keep track of your thoughts, make lists, or write a poem. It's up to you.
- Your character will automatically drink untainted water in their main inventory. This can be toggled in the Accessibilty tab of Options.
- Some zombies have maps made by ex-survivors. They may or may not lead you to treasure.
- If you press the Shout button while crouched, you will whisper, attracting zombies from a smaller radius than a shout.
- Equipping a tissue or toilet roll will muffle the sound of your sneezes and coughs.
- Think an area is free of zombies? Shout to make sure.
- Your footwear will change your movement speed, your stomping damage, and how much noise you make when moving.
- Boil Tainted water to make it safe to drink. Tainted water can be used to water crops with no penalty.
- Wearing dirty or bloody clothes will make wound infection (but NOT Knox Virus Infection) more likely. Wash them with water, or in a washing machine.
- Normal zombies too slow? You can turn on Sprinters in Sandbox.
- Want a different challenge? Make your own fun by playing Sandbox.
- Food can be frozen in a freezer - if it's powered - to last longer. Most frozen food has to be thawed before eating.
- Sneaking uses more energy than walking, but makes you less likely to be spotted.
- Fliers will almost always reveal a specific location on your map, if you click the button towards the bottom while reading it.
- Pick up or dismantle furniture using the "wooden locker" icon in the upper left.
- Using long melee weapons will exhaust you quicker than using short ones.
- Did you know you can read the back of retail VHS tapes?
- Equip an instrument, whistle, or loudhailer to give your shout more radius or a different sound.
- Standing near a large amount of dead bodies can make you sick.
- Looking for a quick game with lots of action? Try Last Stand in Challenge mode.
- Remember to check your corners.
- Putting items in an equipped backpack reduces their weight. Bug
- An animal inside a Livestock Zone is automatically assigned to it, and will not leave even if the Zone is unfenced.
- Smaller animals can be picked up and moved directly. Larger animals must be led around on a rope, and can be tied to a post/tree.
- An animal will accept you the longer you stay with it. Petting it also helps (and reduces your stress).
- Animals will eat food in their Zones, whether in a feeding trough or directly on the ground.
- Animals can drink from puddles, troughs, or be given water from a container directly.
- Some items can only be crafted on a table.
- Some recipes are automatically learned if your skills are high enough.
- Having a crafting recipe at hand will allow you to craft the item at one skill level lower.
- Muscle strain from melee weapons can be lowered by increasing Strength , and/or your skill in that weapon type.
- Double click a clothing item or weapon in your inventory to wear or equip it.

## Trivia

- "Also try Minecraft!" is a reference to a Minecraft loading screen Easter egg, "Also try Project Zomboid" .
- Telecorn refers to an old running joke that Corn would teleport the player to random places.
- "They're coming to get you." quick tip is a likely reference to an iconic quote from the 1968 Night of the Living Dead , one of the first zombie movies and the one that popularized the slow shambler genre.
- "Beware of bathrooms." refers to a lot of survivor stories tied to bathrooms specifically, a lot of them potentially killing players.
- Quick tips still refer to encumbrance as "weight". Bug
- There is a typo in the Dark Wallow Lake being called "Darkwallow". Bug
