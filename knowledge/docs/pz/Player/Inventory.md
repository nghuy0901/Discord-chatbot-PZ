# Inventory

The inventory is a collection of items either worn on the player , kept as possessions, or stored in a container in the game world. The player's inventory can be used to equip, wear, inspect or consume items that have been collected within the game world.

## Mechanics

By default, the left inventory window at the top of the screen is the inventory of the player character. The inventory displays the items the player has collected or is currently wearing or equipped. In the left area of the window is the list of items within the inventory of the selected container on the right side of the window. The first container shown on the right is the container that represents the player character. Each other item within the right side of the window are containers that can themselves store items, like backpacks and fanny packs .

Clicking on containers found throughout the game world will also display the container's inventory; by default in the right hand window. Items can be moved from inventory to container by drag and dropping or utilizing the context menu. Multiple containers may be shown on the right side of the window; these are simply containers that the player is within range of. The floor/ground is also itself a container that can be managed in the same way.

#### Encumbrance

Items added to inventories add to the encumbrance of the container. All items in the game have an encumbrance value; the value is based on the item's weight and size. The current encumbrance of the container, along with the maximum limit are displayed at the top right of each inventory window.

Going over the encumbrance limit of the player results in debuffs to the player that must be managed. The limit of the player can be increased via the strength skill. Equipping items or putting items into backpacks that the player can wear will reduce their encumbrance. Actions taken in the inventory often require some time in the game world: progress is represented with a green bar above the player's head.

### Equipment

The player can choose items to equip from the inventory in either their primary or secondary slot, or both in some cases, such as the axe or baseball bat . Equipment can include guns, bats, slicing and stabbing weapons, tissues (to muffle sneezes during a cold) bags, hammers, trowels, and gardening spray cans. Equipment that is actively equipped will show at the bottom of the item list of the player's inventory.

### Clothing

Within the game world or on zombies, players can find clothing items . In the inventory, the player can wear that clothing by right-clicking the item and equipping it. Clothing keeps the player warm in the cold or cooler in the heat. Individual pieces of clothing might also provide bite and scratch defense, with each kind of clothing giving different effects.

### Food

Food can be eaten and some food can be created from the right click menu of the inventory window, like stews and soups.

### Storage

Containers are either found within the game world, such as shelves and cabinets, bags that the player can wear/use, or player crafted storage.

All bags must either be held in the primary/secondary slot, or alternatively on the back or waist, in the case of the backpacks or fanny packs. Each type of bag has different encumbrance reduction statistics, reducing the encumbrance of whatever is stored in it. Unlike the player inventory, bags have a hard limit on the amount they can store. Items which do not fit into the limit of the container cannot be stored within the bag.
