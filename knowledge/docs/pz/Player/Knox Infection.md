# Knox Infection

Lore Businesses • Metagame • Outfit • Survivor • Knox Event • Knox Infection • Raleigh Outbreak • Randomized stories

Spoiler warning! The following contains unhidden spoilers pertaining to the story of Project Zomboid . Proceed at your own risk.

“ The Knox Infection's final stage is one of violence. — Kirsty Cormick

Knox Infection (commonly known as zombie infection ) is a highly contagious and invariably fatal disease contracted only by humans. This disease is very different from the common cold , food poisoning or wound infection . It is the main catalyst for the events that unfold in Project Zomboid , dubbed the Knox Event .

## Story

### Origin

Very little is known about the origin of the disease, other than that its first known outbreak began in the state of Kentucky, USA , with the largest outbreaks specifically in the city of Louisville . There are several in-game rumors about the origin, such as misfolded proteins (prions) in the meat of Spiffo's , bioweapons testing gone wrong, and God's punishment for the sins of man. There is also the speculation of terrorism being the cause of the virus.

### Initial outbreak

The exact date of the initial outbreak is unknown and estimated to be between July 2 and July 6, 1993 (one week prior to the start of the game, on standard settings). The military quickly set up an "exclusion zone" around Louisville before it managed to spread further, temporarily containing the outbreak. At first, the government portrayed the outbreak to be flu-like, causing panic and confusion among the infected. The infection is known to spread through fluid contact: scratches and bites.

Five days after the blockade went up, the WHO grounded all non-military or non-medical international flights, which led to mass panic throughout the nation and a disgruntled response from the president. The response and lack of clarity from the United States government led to international leaders condemning America, with the UK premier criticizing the president's "near lack of clarity". A photo from West Point is leaked out of the Exclusion Zone, with an image of a man covered in blood and bites, one arm missing, in a street crowded with the dead.

### Second wave

After eight days, shots and explosions occur at the border of the exclusion zone, catching the attention of infected civilians, leading to hundreds, maybe thousands, breaking through the blockade. Within two days, the infection spread North to Louisville , littering the streets with the dead along the way. As a last resort, the military blew the bridges across the Ohio River, leaving those left in Louisville to fend for themselves.

Something is released with the wave of the dead, as people begin showing symptoms of the infection without coming into contact with any infected. The Knox Infection quickly spreads throughout the USA, causing a breakdown of society, and even starts to spread across the ocean to Europe, Asia, and Africa despite the WHO's lockdown order on American flights.

## Pathology

Initially, the infection seemed to only spread via fluids – typically blood and saliva – and thus the transmission rate was relatively low. Approximately a week into the Knox Event , however, it mutates, and an airborne strain develops, which then spreads much more rapidly after the incident at the exclusion zone border and the breach thereof.

### Diagnosis

Those infected will suffer influenza-like symptoms for up to three days after contracting the illness and eventually succumb, after which they will then reanimate within an hour after death. The infection could also apparently be detected via blood tests, which would mean that Knox Infection is a virus rather than a prion, as the only way to truly confirm prion disease is a brain autopsy.

### Pathophysiology

The pathophysiology of the infection is never mentioned during the events of Project Zomboid, though radio interviews suggest that infected individuals become confused and fatigued before death and reanimation. On WBLN News , Professor Peter Endsleigh draws a connection to Mad Cow Disease, although based on the rapid spread of the disease and its ability to be detected via blood test, Endsleigh's prion theory may be difficult to believe. Available evidence suggests that the disease is more likely to be of viral origin.

There are no known cases of surviving the infection, and therefore, the direct effects and specific immune response (if any) are unknown.

### Immunity

Whilst some individuals (including all player characters ) are immune to the airborne strain of the infection, there are no known cases of immunity to both the airborne and the fluid-contact strains.

## Gameplay

### Infection

In-game, the only way to contract the Knox Infection is to be attacked by a zombie, in the form of scratches, lacerations, and bites. Any injuries that are not inflicted by zombies will never cause the player to get infected. While being bitten guarantees contracting the disease, it is possible to survive scratches and lacerations from zombies.

The game does not outright tell the player if they are infected with the Knox Infection. However, the symptoms will manifest within hours after intially contracting the disease, which are stress and queasiness , along with rising body temperature due to said queasiness. These statuses are not brought upon exclusively by the Knox Infection, but if the player has them for seemingly no reason and especially after being recently attacked by a zombie, it is almost certainly the Knox Infection.

As symptoms worsen, the player's health will degrade faster, which can only be slowed by remaining well-fed , hydrated and by the absence of a cold . It is not possible to halt or reverse the progression of the Knox Infection, nor survive the fever. There are no cures or treatments of any kind for the disease.

Characters who have contracted the Knox Infection will reanimate within a minute or two of their death, and will do so regardless of their actual cause of death .

### Hypochondriac

The disabled hypochondriac trait will cause the player to occasionally get the symptoms of the zombie infection after receiving a scratch, without actually being infected. The progression is identical to the Knox Infection. However, once the 'fake infection' reaches its peak, the infection will dissipate as the player begins to recover. The rate of recovery is much quicker than the infection's progression. However, this trait is currently commented out (as of Build 41.65) and cannot be added to a character without being modded back in.

## Sandbox settings

In Custom Sandbox settings, there are three attributes of the Knox Infection itself that players can freely adjust and modify to their personal taste. Transmission , Infection Mortality , and Reanimate Time . These attributes are located in the Zombies tab of Custom Sandbox settings.

### Transmission

This attribute refers to the methods at which the Knox Infection could be transmitted. There are four settings, as shown below.

- Blood and Saliva (Default) - The player can contract the disease via scratches, lacerations, and bites from zombies.
- Saliva Only - The player can only contract the disease via bites. It is impossible to get infected from scratches or lacerations.
- Everyone's Infected - Anybody that dies will always get up as a zombie, even if they were never attacked by a zombie.
- None - The player can never contract the Knox Infection. Not even if they were bitten.

### Infection Mortality

This attribute refers to how long it will take for the player to die after contracting the Knox Infection. There are five settings: Instant , 0-30 Seconds , 0-1 Minutes , 0-12 Hours , 2-3 Days (Default) , 1-2 Weeks , and Never . The setting of "Never" means that the player has a possibility of surviving the disease, not that they would never contract it.

### Reanimate Time

This attribute refers to how long it will take for the corpses of Knox Infection infectees to get up as a zombie after death. Note that the corpses of uninfected persons will never get up, unless the Transmission attribute was set to Everyone's Infected. This attribute has five settings: Instant , 0-30 Seconds , 0-1 Minutes (Default) , 0-12 Hours , 2-3 Days , and 1-2 Weeks .

## Popular myths

Below are some commonly believed myths in the game, along with clarifications:

**"Survival from a zombie bite is possible":** Originally in older versions, as noted in patch 0.1.6a , there was a small 4% chance to survive a zombie bite. This was changed in later updates, making a zombie bite 100% lethal.

**"Recovery from infection through Rest and Nourishment":** This misconception might have stemmed from the hypochondriac trait, general food sickness, or confusion with wound infection. However, it is impossible to survive the 'real' infection once contracted.

**"Disinfection can eliminate the infection":** Disinfecting a wound post-bite does not eliminate the infection. Once infected, it is not possible to reverse or halt the progression of the disease.

**"Preventing reanimation by dying prematurely (e.g., drinking bleach )":** This myth is based on a bug in versions prior to Build 41.5 where dying before the infection could set in prevented reanimation. This allowed players to retrieve their possessions without having to deal with their zombified former selves.

**"Bloodied-hand zombies are more infectious":** While it may seem logical, zombies with blood on their hands are not more likely to infect through scratches. Such zombies usually have bloodied hands due to climbing through broken windows.

**"Infection risk from broken glass":** Infection in the game can only be contracted through direct zombie attacks. It's not possible to get infected by climbing through broken windows without clearing the glass first, although regular wound infections can occur, provided the wounds are not properly treated.

## Trivia

- Infection lore is heavily based on George A. Romero zombies, with some very minor alterations.
- Children can also be infected with the Knox Infection and become zombies, mentioned on day 6 and day 7 on NNR Radio and day 6 on KnoxTalk Radio , but Project Zomboid will not have zombie children in the game itself.
- The entire planet Earth is infected and completely overrun by July 28th of 1993, according to the Knox Event timeline, with currently only small groups of immune survivors remaining.
- There is an unmarked secret government facility deep in the woods on the southern edge of the map, which seems to have some medical and animal testing facilities and is a massive hotspot for the infected. In the basement of this building there is a large laboratory complex, which also houses a morgue and a tomography scanner. The laboratory is connected to the caves, which include, among other things, a makeshift camp. Whether it is related to the outbreak or not is currently unknown.
- Given that the infection can be detected via a same-day blood test seemingly much like other viruses, it makes it likely that the infection isn't a prion disease. The only way to confirm a person is infected with prion disease is with a lengthy brain autopsy.
