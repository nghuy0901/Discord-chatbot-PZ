# Starting location

Player Moodle • Skill • Trait • Occupation • Inventory • Nutrition • Sleep • Condition • Health • Knox Infection

The player 's starting location is determined by their chosen occupation and their selection from the five starting towns ( Echo Creek , Muldraugh , Riverside , Rosewood , or West Point ).

The Chef , Construction Worker , Doctor , Fire Officer , Park Ranger , Police Officer , DIY Expert , Security Guard , and Custom Occupation occupations have explicitly defined starting locations. [verify]

Any other occupation behaves as "Custom Occupation" for the purpose of determining starting location.

The tables below describe the different occupations and the spawn chances at each when starting a new game with that occupation. For blank entries, the occupation cannot spawn at that location.
