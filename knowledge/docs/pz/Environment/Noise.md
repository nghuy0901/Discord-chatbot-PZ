# Noise

Game mechanics Zombie • Health • Crafting • Building • Fire • AI • Noise • Weather • Erosion • Electricity • Metagame • Helicopter

Noise attracts all zombies in a general proximity in the general direction of the player's presence. The level of hearing zombies possesses can be changed in the sandbox settings before starting the game. Some actions generate more noise than others, and therefore are more dangerous to perform. There are many actions that make noise that will attract zombies . These include the following:

- Sprinting; Base Volume 14
- Walking; Base Volume 7
- Sneaking; Base Volume 3.5 See also Lightfooted and Nimble
- Fire ; Volume 20
- Televisions , radios and alarms .
- Discharging a Firearm , Volumes between 30 for a toy cap gun and 200 for a shotgun
- Chopping down a tree.
- Using the "shout" function; Volume 30 (x3 with megaphone)
- Using the whisper function; Volume 6 (x3 with megaphone)
- Smashing windows , doors , and walls.
- (De)construction ; Volume 15
- Knocking down a door .
- Setting off a burglar alarm ; Volume 600
- Honking a horn or setting off a vehicle alarm; Volume 150
- Other zombies knocking at a door or window ; Volume 20
- Sneezing and coughing Volume 40 (unless muffled with a tissue ).
- Having a generator running;  Volumes between 20 and 25 (see generator type)
- Driving and idling a vehicle ; Volumes between 55 for a small car in decent repair and 220 for a large vehicle without a muffler.
- Opening and closing doors
- Alarm Clocks Radius 15
- Watch/Pocketwatch  Radius 7

Noise can be muffled by walls, doors, and distance between the generator of the noise and zombies .

Depending on the game mode selected, or sandbox settings selected, the metagame will have helicopters fly overhead, or random gunshots fired to move hordes of zombies around the map.

Note that there is some nuance in the game's code about priority of competing sounds between variables Radius (raw power) and Volume (attractiveness to zombies), in most cases these are numerically identical, for instance the helicopter has 500 radius and volume. This distinction however means that some noises can treated as more alluring to zombies than their basic radius would suggest. As an example a vehicle lightbar/siren has 100 radius and only 60 Volume this means that a zombie will sometimes ignore a siren to chase a coughing survivor, same goes for gunfire.
