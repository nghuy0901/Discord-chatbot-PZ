# Vehicle

Vehicle Ambulance • Chevalier Cerise Wagon • Chevalier Cossette • Chevalier D6 • Chevalier Dart • Chevalier Nyala • Chevalier Primani • Chevalier Step Van • Dash Bulldriver • Dash Elite • Dash Rancher • Franklin All-Terrain • Franklin Valuline • Franklin Valuline (6-seater) • LBMW Radio Van • Masterson Horizon • Mercia Lang 4000 • Race Car • Horse trailer • Livestock Trailer • Trailer • Trailer (advert) • Trailer (cover)

Vehicles were added in Build 39 and are the main way to traverse Knox Country . Their usage can vary from transportation of items and players to killing zeds and mobile houses. Primarily vehicles can be found on parking lots, driveways, along bridges or major highways and as a part of a randomized story . Their condition may vary, ranging from pristine to useless wreckage.

## Types

Vehicles have three distinct types, standard, heavy-duty and sports. Usually, each vehicle type has a designated zone for spawning, sports cars spawning in rich neighborhoods, heavy-duty vehicles spawning next to industrial buildings and so on. A recipe magazine is essential for adding, removing, or repairing parts specific to each vehicle, unless the player is a mechanic or has the vehicle knowledge trait.

Note: players with vehicle knowledge trait still need the Magazine: Laines Performance Auto Manual magazine for sports vehicles.

- Standard , learned by the Magazine: Laines Standard Auto Manual
- Heavy-Duty , learned by the Magazine: Laines Commercial Auto Manual
- Sports , learned by the Magazine: Laines Performance Auto Manual

There are currently 14 different vehicle models available, as well as three types of trailers. However, there are multiple variants of some vehicles, such as the Fossoil Chevalier D6 , and the Police Chevalier Nyala . Vehicles belonging to a certain profession will have relevant items spawn inside. For example, an ambulance may contain medical supplies.

## Controls

### Radial menu

Most of the vehicle interactions can be performed from the vehicle's radial menu, accessed by pressing V .

#### Inside

- Vehicle radial menu when inside From inside, this includes ignition, radio, and headlights.
- Switch Seat – displays the panel allowing to choose a different seat.
- No key. Electrical level 1 and mechanics level 2 are needed to hotwire or Hotwire Engine – allows to hotwire a vehicle if keys are not in ignition or on the player.
- Turn Headlights on or Turn Headlights off – turns the headlights on or off respectively.
- Turn AC/Heater on or Turn AC/Heater off – displays the temperature panel of AC/Heater, allowing to adjust the temperature inside the vehicle. is displayed instead when the temperature dial is below half.
- Horn – horns a horn.
- Lightbar – displays the panel of the lightbar if vehicle has one.
- Device Options – displays the panel of the installed radio if installed, can only be accessed from a front seat. is present in the game files, but it never appear, even if radio is off or muted.
- Open Window or Close Window – opens or closes the player's seat window if present. Opened window makes the inside temperature the same as the outside temperature, allow to shoot and allows zombies to attack the player.
- Lock all doors or Unlock all doors – locks or unlocks all doors, can be used from any seat, even the ones in police cars.
- Vehicle Mechanics open the Vehicle Mechanics panel.
- Not tired enough to sleep. or Sleep – allows the player to sleep in the vehicle.
- Exit Vehicle – exits vehicle through a closest door.

### Outside

From outside, one can manage doors, hood, trunk, smash windows and more.

- Vehicle Mechanics open the Vehicle Mechanics panel.
- Enter Vehicle – displays the panel allowing to choose a seat.
- Open Hood/Door/Trunk or Close Hood/Door/Trunk – opens or close the hood, door and trunk. Effects are currently not visible in regular cars.
- Smash (Rear) Windshield/Front/Rear/Left/Right Window – smashes the window, leaving broken glass below.

#### Refueling

A vehicle can be refueled by right-clicking on it and selecting refuel , the option to siphon gas is available if the player has an empty gas can . Vehicles can be refueled using a container with gas, gas can or at a gas pump , provided the gas pump is connected to a power source. If the option to refuel from the gas pump does not appear, the gas cap may be on the other side of the car, blocked or too far from reach; try repositioning the vehicle so that the gas cap is on the same side as the gas pump, fuel indicator shows on which side cap is with an arrow.

#### Towing

The player can tow any car (even car wrecks ) by driving a second vehicle to its front or rear, walking between them, facing either vehicle, pressing the vehicle radial menu (V), and selecting the plus sign. While towing doesn't require any other equipment, the towing mechanic has a few factors most players won't consider that determine the quality of the ability to tow.

**Tire pressure:** The tire pressure of both the towing vehicle and the vehicle being towed affect the speed of which the vehicle can be towed.

**Traits:** The Sunday driver trait will negatively impact the player's ability to tow vehicles greatly, currently the has vehicle towing is being heavily affected by acceleration, with Sunday driver heavily decreasing player acceleration. Conversely, the speed demon trait will positively impact the player's ability to tow vehicles greatly for the same reason.

**Weight to engine power ratio:** The overall weight of both vehicles does play a factor in the towing speed. However, the amount of weight required to make any noticeable difference would require the towed vehicle to be stripped of everything save for the tires/brakes/shocks – even then, the difference is fairly small.

### Dashboard

Displays the current status of the vehicle. Some icons can be activated by clicking with the left mouse button .

**Indicator colors:** 

- Gray : Off, unlocked or no info.
- Green : On or locked.
- Orange : Engine starting or low fuel.
- Yellow : Not all doors are locked.
- Red : Broken or not present.

- Engine Status : Shows if the engine is off, starting, or on. : The engine is off (not ignited). : The engine is currently in the process of ignition. : The engine is on (ignited). : The engine is broken or not present.
- Battery : Represents the battery's health. : The battery is off, no info is given. : The battery is charged and functional. : The battery is discharged or not present.
- Door Locks : Used to lock or unlock doors. Does not require car keys. : The car doors are unlocked and can be opened without car keys. : Some car doors are unlocked and some locked. : The car doors are locked and can only be opened using car keys.
- Headlights : Turns lights on or off. Does not require car keys. : The headlights are off. : The headlights are on. : The headlights are broken, damaged or not present.
- Heater/AC : Controls the heating or air conditioner. Does not require car keys. There's also an unused icon for air conditioning, present in the game files. : The heater is off. : The heater is on.
- Trunk Lock : Locks or unlocks the trunk. Does not require car keys. : The trunk is unlocked and can be opened without car keys. : The trunk is locked and can only be opened using car keys.
- Ignition : This is where the car key goes. It's safe to leave the key here; it won’t drain the battery. : The car key is not inserted into the key slot. Therefore, the engine is not ignited and is off. : The car key is inserted into the key slot, but is not turned. Therefore, the engine is not ignited and is off. : The car key is inserted into the key slot and turned. Therefore, the engine is ignited and is on. : The car key is not present, but the vehicle has been hot-wired. There is no way to tell if the engine is ignited or not by looking at the key slot in this state.
- Fuel Gauge : Gauge that measures the current amount of fuel in the gas tank . : The gas tank has 15% or more fuel. : The gas tank has  less than 15% of fuel. : The gas tank has less than 5% of fuel.
- Gearshift Position : Displays the current gear the vehicle is in. P : Parking (engine off) N : Neutral / Idle (engine on) R : Reverse driving 1 : Gear shift position 1 2 : Gear shift position 2 3 : Gear shift position 3 (partially accessible with Sunday Driver trait) 4 : Gear shift position 4 (not accessible with Sunday Driver trait) 5 : Gear shift position 5 (not accessible with Sunday Driver trait)

## Car key

Car keys only work with one vehicle. They allow you to:

- Start the engine
- Open the doors from the outside
- Open the trunk from the outside

Where car keys can be found:

- Ground: near the car on the ground.
- Nearby buildings: in boxes or drawers inside close houses or shops.
- In the vehicle: in the ignition or glove box.
- On the body of a nearby zombie: can randomly spawn on the body of the nearest killed zombies.

When the player is close to a car and have its key, a key picture shows up over your character. This means the player can use and drive that car.

### Hot-wiring

If the player can't find a car's key, it can still be started by hot-wiring. For this, a player needs:

- Level 1 in electrical skill
- Level 2 in mechanics skill

- Burglar occupation

How to hot-wire:

- Get inside the car (driver seat).
- Open the radial menu.
- Look for "Hotwire Engine" (this replaces "Start Engine").
- The usual ways to start the car (like clicking the ignition or pressing W ) won't work without a key.

When a car is hot-wired:

- The ignition icon on the dashboard changes.
- The player see the key picture up over their character.

Note: Be careful in a multiplayer game. Any player can access a hot-wired car.

If doors are locked, you can:

- Break the window.
- Remove the window through the car's mechanics menu.

Trying and failing to hot-wire:

- Doesn't break the car or your tools.
- Can make noise and draw attention.
- Remember, cars in better shape are trickier to hot-wire.

### Vehicle Noise

##### 1. Vehicle base (From the table above):

Loudness is between 55 to 110 for vanilla vehicles

#### 2. Muffler Modifier:

- No muffler: Full loudness
- Damaged muffler (50%): 75% loudness
- Perfect muffler (100%): 50% loudness

##### 3. Speed modifiers

Speed modifiers are based on the RPM of the vehicle and are approximately linear.

Stationary with motor on: 20% radius

Reversing around 2000 RPM 40% radius.

Normal Driving around 4000 RPM: 100% radius

(Theoretical) Driving at 7000 RPM: 175% radius

Minimum sound radius is 8 squares

##### 4. Sound Generation (every second while engine running):

Engines create all sorts of noises, not all of which consistently bring in zombies from all directions, every second the game checks four different sounds, each with a minimum of 8 radius.

- Sound 4 (automatic every second ) : radius = Actual Volume / 6
- Sound 3 (1/10 chance per second) : radius = Actual Volume / 4)
- Sound 2 (1/30 chance per second) : radius = Actual Volume  / 2)
- Sound 1 (1/120 chance per second) : radius = Actual Volume

So a vehicle will, on average once every two minutes attract zombies in a wide area but this is not consistent.

Lastly note that vehicle noises, though loud, are not as attractive to zombies as some quieter noises. A zombie will often prefer to chase a shout or a sneeze over an engine.

## Common issues

**Vehicle disappearing after game crash:** When driving a vehicle during a crash or closing/exiting the game incorrectly, upon reload can result in the vehicle either being at the location it was first entered or where it was during the crash. Given that the player may spawn anywhere from start to any point along the route driven, this can be problematic.

**Slow-motion:** Often the game can appear to run in slow-motion (low FPS rate) when driving at higher speeds. The zoom level can affect this and can be used as a means of testing if it is an FPS issue or a different problem. Lowering the resolution, reducing the number of 3D characters, closing any background programs, making Project Zomboid a high priority in task manager, or performing a clean install of graphics drivers may resolve this issue.
