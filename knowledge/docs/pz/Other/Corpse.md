# Corpse

Items Ammo • Animal part • Appearance • Camping • Clothing • Communication • Container • Cooking • Corpse • Electronics • Explosive • Fire source • Fishing • Fluid container • Food • Fuel • Gardening • Household • Instrument • Junk • Light source • Literature • Material • Medical • Memento • Security • Sports • Tool • Trap • Vehicle maintenance • Weapon • Weapon part • Debug

Corpse as items can spawn naturally during world generation as dead survivors or as the result of the player killing a zombie or animal . Dead survivors can be found as part of a survivor house or survivor story and very rarely in the trunk of vehicles . Corpses can be found with a range of items such as clothing or weapons . Corpses can be burned with 100ml of gasoline or on a campfire , or they can be buried in a grave .

One of the common misconceptions about corpses is their ability to attract more zombies after some time. While zeds can be found feasting on corpses, decomposing bodies do not lure them, so the most negative effect of having bodies around you is the corpse sickness .
