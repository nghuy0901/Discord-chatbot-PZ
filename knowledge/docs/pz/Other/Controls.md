# Controls

Meta Controls • Multiplayer • Survival Guide • Game modes • Frequently Asked Questions • Dedicated server • Server settings • Custom Sandbox • Discord

The default controls are displayed below; most (not all) key bindings can be customized in the game options along with gamepad/controller configuration. In order to customize a key binding, the desired key change must be left-clicked , followed by the desired keystroke. If done correctly, the desired key will be displayed within the box.

## General

### RMB
- **Key:** RMB
- **Function:** Contextual Menu
- **Description:** Pressing will display the contextual menu, which will contain all available interactions with that object.
### LMB
- **Key:** LMB
- **Function:** Select
- **Description:** This is context specific and will vary depending on what it interacts with, such as picking up items, opening and closing of doors, windows curtains and light switches.
### = / MSW
- **Key:** = / MSW
- **Function:** Zoom-in
- **Description:** Zooms the camera in closer to the player, decreasing the field of view.
### - / MSW
- **Key:** - / MSW
- **Function:** Zoom-out
- **Description:** Zooms the camera out farther from the player, increasing the field of view.
### ESC
- **Key:** ESC
- **Function:** Main Menu
- **Description:** Pauses a single-player game. Displays the pause menu, allowing the player to adjust options , exit game, etc. Re-enables HUD (if hidden).

## Player Control

### W
- **Key:** W
- **Function:** Forward
- **Description:** The basic directional movement of the player. Holding both a horizontal and vertical movement key will cause the player to move diagonally. Pressing the RMB and selecting move here , will cause the player to move to that location.
### S
- **Key:** S
- **Function:** Backward
### A
- **Key:** A
- **Function:** Left
### D
- **Key:** D
- **Function:** Right
### L. SHIFT
- **Key:** L. SHIFT
- **Function:** Run
- **Description:** Press and hold (depending on the toggle run option ) to increase movement speed, at the cost of generating more noise and exertion . Double tapping will cause the player to sprint.
### L. ALT
- **Key:** L. ALT
- **Function:** Sprint
- **Description:** Press and hold (depending on the toggle sprint option ) to vastly increase movement speed, at the cost of generating significant amount of noise and high exertion . Sprinting into a solid object will result in falling over backwards. Sprinting through trees and over fences increases chance of tripping, causing damage and dropping equipped items.
### E
- **Key:** E
- **Function:** Interact
- **Description:** Pressing once will perform the first action on the contextual menu, without displaying it. (e.g. Opening a door, window...) Holding will perform the follow up action on the contextual menu (e.g. Going through a window after opening). Tapping rapidly will make an urgent action , causing the player to take drastic measures (e.g. Smashing a window with their arm).
### ⇧ Shift + E
- **Key:** ⇧ Shift + E
- **Function:** Special Interact
- **Description:** Most of the time this does nothing. Some objects have special interactions: Pressing this near a window will open/close the curtains. This list is not comprehensive; please add to it if more are discovered.
### ESC
- **Key:** ESC
- **Function:** Cancel Action
- **Description:** Cancels any action the player is currently performing.

## Combat

### C
- **Key:** C
- **Function:** Sneak (Crouch)
- **Description:** Generates less noise . Reduces visibility. Crouching behind objects reduces visibility further (e.g. fences). Toggle: pressing will perform the action until pressed again.
### L. CTRL / RMB
- **Key:** L. CTRL / RMB
- **Function:** Aim
- **Description:** Holding causes the player to face in the direction of the mouse cursor . Moving while held causes the player to generate less noise . The longer it's held down, the more accurate the attack will be.
### LMB
- **Key:** LMB
- **Function:** Fire/Attack
- **Description:** Pressing will cause the player to fire or attack in the direction the player is facing.
### SPACE
- **Key:** SPACE
- **Function:** Melee
- **Description:** Causes the player to perform an unarmed melee shove , or stomp in the direction the player is facing.
### X
- **Key:** X
- **Function:** Rack Firearm
- **Description:** Racks a round into the chamber of a firearm, making it ready to fire. A round will be removed from the firearm if there is already a round in the chamber.
### R
- **Key:** R
- **Function:** Reload Weapon
- **Description:** Press: inserts/removes a magazine. Swaps current magazine with a full magazine (if available) Removes current magazine and fills (if available and not full) Removes magazine (if full) Hold: opens radial menu. Only options that are available will appear: "Rack", "Eject Magazine", "Load Bullets into spare Magazine"
### L. ALT
- **Key:** L. ALT
- **Function:** Manual Floor Attack
- **Description:** Forces targetting prone enemies instead of standing enemies.

## Vehicle

- **Key:** Spacebar
- **Function:** Hand brake
- **Description:** Turns off the cruise control if it is on.
### SHIFT + W
- **Key:** SHIFT + W
- **Function:** Increase Cruise Control Speed
- **Description:** If cruise control is off, this will turn it back on at the previous cruise control speed, or the current speed if none exists. When cruise control is enabled, pressing this will increase the cruise control speed by 5 MPH.
### SHIFT + S
- **Key:** SHIFT + S
- **Function:** Decrease Cruise Control Speed
- **Description:** If cruise control is off, this will turn it back on at the previous cruise control speed, or the current speed if none exists. When cruise control is enabled, pressing this will decrease the cruise control speed by 5 MPH.

### Other controls

### V
- **Key:** V
- **Function:** Vehicle Radial Menu
- **Description:** Displays the vehicle radial menu, which can be used to perform all the vehicle's available actions, see vehicle page for details.
### F
- **Key:** F
- **Function:** Toggle Vehicle Headlights
- **Description:** Toggles the headlights between an on and off state.
### N
- **Key:** N
- **Function:** Start Vehicle Engine
- **Description:** Attempts to start the engine, either by key or hot-wiring . Using W while inside a turned-off vehicle will also ignite the engine.
### O
- **Key:** O
- **Function:** Heater
- **Description:** Toggles the heater panel, which can be used to adjust the vehicle's heating and cooling.
### U
- **Key:** U
- **Function:** Info
- **Description:** Displays the vehicle mechanics/info, which can take a few seconds – the player must be outside the vehicle. Otherwise, they'll be removed from the vehicle before opening the panel.
### E
- **Key:** E
- **Function:** Enter/Exit Vehicle
- **Description:** The vehicle can be entered when standing next to a door (displaying a green square) or through the radial menu. The player exits the vehicle from the side based on their current seat.
### Z
- **Key:** Z
- **Function:** Switch Seat
- **Description:** Displays the vehicle's seats, showing the player's and any occupied seats. The player can move seats by either clicking the desired seat, or by pressing the designated numbered hotkey shown on the seat. The player may also choose which seat to exit the vehicle from this menu.
### Q
- **Key:** Q
- **Function:** Horn
- **Description:** Sounds the vehicle's horn until the key is released, or if using the radial menu, it will sound for 2 seconds. Attracts any zombies towards the vehicle.

## UI

The user interface , or just UI , displays information to the player, some of which is interactable.

### F1
- **Key:** F1
- **Function:** Toggle Survival Guide
- **Description:** Toggles the survival guide , which is an brief in-game contextual tutorial describing certain aspects of Project Zomboid.
### V
- **Key:** V
- **Function:** Toggle UI
- **Description:** Toggles the HUD. Ideal for taking uncluttered screenshots. Note that this will not function when nearby a vehicle, and will instead trigger the vehicle radial menu.
### K
- **Key:** K
- **Function:** Display FPS
- **Description:** Toggles the display of FPS (Frames Per Second) in the top-left corner of the screen.
### F10
- **Key:** F10
- **Function:** Take screenshot
- **Description:** Pressing will take a screenshot and store it in the \Users\USERNAME\Zomboid\Screenshots folder. There is no in-game confirmation that a screenshot has been taken, therefore the player should be aware that each keystroke will save a new screenshot.
### J
- **Key:** J
- **Function:** Info Panel
- **Description:** Toggles the info panel. Providing: Portrait view of character Name Weight - displays a chevron indicating whether the player's weight is increasing or decreasing. Occupation Traits Hair - including options to cut hair. Training Materials - list of books , magazines , CDs and VHSes that the played has read, watched and listened to. Zombies killed
### L
- **Key:** L
- **Function:** Skill Panel
- **Description:** Displays all the players skills.
### P
- **Key:** P
- **Function:** Protection Panel
- **Description:** Displays the player's protection rating for each body part.

### Inventory

### I
- **Key:** I
- **Function:** Toggle Inventory
- **Description:** Toggles between the inventory windows being displayed and hidden.
### RMB
- **Key:** RMB
- **Function:** Display Contextual Menu
- **Description:** Can be used on any item in the player's or a container's inventory to display their uses - whether they can be eaten , drunk , equipped or used in crafting recipes .
### LMB
- **Key:** LMB
- **Function:** Highlighting
- **Description:** This will highlight the selected item. Holding and dragging will select all items within the selection box. After selecting an item, Ctrl + LMB can be used when selecting a second item, highlighting the second item while keeping the initial one highlighted. Alternatively, ⇧ Shift + LMB can be used when selecting the second item, also highlighting all items between the two selections.

## Controller

It is possible to use a gamepad rather than the standard mouse and keyboard, which is ideal for local 4 player split-screen co-op.

To use a controller, it must first be connected and installed correctly in the operating system.

Any controller can be tested from the controller tab in the game options , to ensure all buttons are functioning correctly. The controller sensitivity can also be adjusted there.

While any controller can be used, the game always shows button propmts for Xbox controllers, which may be confusing for some. However, there exist user-created mods that alleviate that.

The controls for a controller are usually context-based and can change depending on the action. However, they will usually be displayed on-screen. The general controls for a controller are displayed below.

### Left stick (press)
- **Left stick:** Left stick (press)
- **Player movement.:** Toggle between walking and running.
### Directional pad
- **Left stick:** Directional pad
- **Player movement.:** Navigate contextual menus.
### Right stick
- **Left stick:** Right stick
- **Player movement.:** Sneak/aim, navigate radial menu.
### A button
- **Left stick:** A button
- **Player movement.:** Primary button to interact with world objects, such as opening doors, windows and turning on/off lights, context specific and will vary depending on what it interacts with.
### B button
- **Left stick:** B button
- **Player movement.:** Secondary button to interact with world objects, such as smashing windows and climbing over fences, close/cancel contextual menus and panels (excluding inventory), expand inventory item lists.
### X button
- **Left stick:** X button
- **Player movement.:** Display contextual menu, displaying available interactions, quick loot/drop item.
### Y button
- **Left stick:** Y button
- **Player movement.:** Open/close inventory menu.
### Left bumper
- **Left stick:** Left bumper
- **Player movement.:** Navigate tabs – rotates through player inventory tabs, cycle build mode.
### Right bumper
- **Left stick:** Right bumper
- **Player movement.:** Navigate tabs – rotates through container inventory tabs.
### Left trigger
- **Left stick:** Left trigger
- **Player movement.:** Shove/stomp (triggered on release).
### Right trigger
- **Left stick:** Right trigger
- **Player movement.:** Fire/attack (triggered on release).
### View/Back
- **Left stick:** View/Back
- **Player movement.:** Press and hold to display radial menu, see below for usage.
### Start
- **Left stick:** Start
- **Player movement.:** Tap once to only pause the game, or tap twice to open pause menu.

View/Back button usage:

- Hold "View/Back" to display radial menu
- Move the "Left stick" to the desired option
- Release "View/Back" before the "Left stick" to select the option

Available options:

- Player info (Info, Skills, Health)
- Crafting
- Move furniture
- Zoom in
- Zoom out
- Pause/Play
- Fast-forward time

## Orientation Control & Zoomed In Mode

In the old versions of the game, there were two more options as well: and .

- Clicking on the crossed arrow symbol would change the orientation direction to isometric mode. Instead of going directly Up with the W key, players would move up-right and so on with other keys.
- Clicking on the pink & blue square with cursor symbol would zoom in the camera – this was very buggy and eventually replaced to generic control instead.
