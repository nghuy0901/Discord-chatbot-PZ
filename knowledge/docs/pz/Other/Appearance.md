# Appearance

Appearance items are used to customize the player 's hairstyle, beard, hair color, and makeup. These can be altered after character creation using items like scissors , razors , hair dye , and makeup.

## Changing appearance

### Hair and beard

Over time, the player's hair and beard (male only) will grow slowly. Beard growth can be seen after 5 days, and hair growth can be seen after 20 days. Both hair and beard length have a maximum length.

The player can modify the style of their hair and beard via the Info panel J , which will display the current styles. Each style requires specific items to be in the player's inventory , which can be seen by hovering over each option. Long hair (fabian), can be tied into a pony tail without requiring any item.

#### Hair dye

Other than the style, the player can also change the color of their hair with hair dye . Hair dye is a fluid that can be found in various colors and mixed to produce new and unique colors.

Hair dye can be used by right-clicking the fluid container with the hair dye and selecting "Dye Hair" or "Dye Beard".

### Makeup

Various items allow the character to apply makeup by right-clicking the item and selecting "Make Up". A "Make Up" window will appear, where the body location and makeup type are chosen. Available makeup types are dependent on the item and body location. Currently, the only makeup items are lipstick , foundation makeup , and eye makeup . Makeup can be removed by washing.

To apply makeup, the player must first be in front of a mirror , have a mirror in their inventory, or be in a vehicle .
