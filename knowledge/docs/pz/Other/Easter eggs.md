# Easter eggs

Meta Controls • Multiplayer • Survival Guide • Game modes • Frequently Asked Questions • Dedicated server • Server settings • Custom Sandbox • Discord

During your time exploring Knox Country , various Easter eggs can be found throughout the area, referencing other games, media, developers etc. Many of these references are in the form of business names, graffiti, items, etc.

## Pop-culture

### Gaming

- On the channel Life and Living TV , there is a program called Exposure Survival. The show's host is named Dean. This is a reference to the game designer Dean Hall . Hall's most famous work being the zombie survival game, DayZ .
- In the woods outside of Muldraugh and Rosewood , a message made out of dirt paths can be found. The message is a love heart with the name Brian. This is a reference to Brian Hicks, creative director of DayZ.
- The name of the ice cream parlor chain Churns-R-Us , is a reference to Chernarus, a fictional Eastern European country in Arma 2 and DayZ.
- The challenge mode ' A Really CD DA ' is a reference to the survival game, Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead .
- Combining an uncooked cake with an orange and a blueberry ( ) creates a cake called 'This Cake is a Lie', referencing the popular phrase from the game Portal . The orange and blueberry represent the colors of the two portals.
- Barrel o' Gun gun store name is likely a reference to popular Doom II: Hell on Earth level, MAP23 "Barrels o' Fun" .
- The abandoned Sunderland Hills Sanatorium is a reference to James Sunderland , protagonist of the survival-horror game Silent Hill 2 , and it's 2024 remake .
- Tim Cain Street in Riverside is a reference to game developer Tim Cain , famous for his creation of the Fallout franchise.
- Sawyer Lane in Riverside is likely referencing game developer Josh Sawyer , famous for his work on role-playing games such as Fallout: New Vegas and Pillars of Eternity .
- The book "Slaves of Arnok: Dwarf Empire" is a reference to colony simulation game Dwarf Fortress .
- Egener EX battery factory is likely a reference to EGX, video game expo .
- The RPG Manual "Damsels & Dangers" is likely a reference to the fantasy tabletop RPG Dungeons & Dragons .
- The RPG Manual "Metalspear 600K" is a reference to the Warhammer 40,000 franchise.
- The Eye of Cthulhu plush toy is a reference to the Eye of Cthulhu boss in 2D sandbox game Terraria .

### Film & TV

- Romero Center for the Performing Arts is a theater in Louisville . The theater's name is a reference to film director George A. Romero . Romero is credited for inventing the modern zombie with his debut film, Night of the Living Dead , earning him multiple different titles such as Godfather of the Dead, Father of the Zombie Film, and the King of the Zombies.
- The Crossroads Mall in Valley Station is a reference to the mall of the same name in the film Dawn of the Dead (2004) . The advertisement though is a reference to the Bone Thugs-n-Harmony song Tha Crossroads .
- Goodman Legal Services is a law firm in Louisville, the name is a reference to Saul Goodman, a lawyer in the TV series Breaking Bad and the spin-off series Better Call Saul .
- Marple & Christie Legal Services in a law firm in Rosewood , the name is a reference to a British TV show Agatha Christie's Marple .
- It can be possible to encounter a zombie wearing a Hockey Mask and with a machete on their back. This is a reference to the immortal slasher Jason Voorhees from the Friday the 13th film franchise.
- In a cemetery in Louisville, there is a pallet of gold, hidden in a mausoleum. The pallet is called " The Ecstacy of Gold " (typo in-game) The pallet's name comes from two songs. The first is a reference to a song of the same name used in the 1966 western film, The Good, the Bad and the Ugly . The song is used in a scene where one of the main characters is desperately searching for a hidden cache of gold coins in a graveyard. The second song is by Metallica, called The Ecsta c y of Gold, also misspelled with two c's.
- Back To The Nurture Chiropractic Services is a chain of chiropractic offices with locations in Louisville and Riverside . The business name is likely a reference to the Back to the Future film trilogy.
- Bateman Office Building in downtown Louisville is a reference to Patrick Bateman , villain protagonist of the American Psycho novel and film adaptation .
- The Shaken and Stirred bar is a reference to a similar catchphrase from the James Bond franchise.
- Louisville State University is referencing British broadcaster and biologist Sir David Attenborough .
- The 28th day is the default day in which the zombie population reaches its peak. This could be referencing the film, 28 Days Later and sequel 28 Weeks Later .
- Mother's Boy VHS and the Waites Motel are a reference to the Psycho franchise .
- One of the lunchbox variants is likely a reference to Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles .
- The Picard hairstyle is likely a reference to Jean-Luc Picard from the science fiction franchise Star Trek .
- Mendy's Eatery is likely a reference to the Seinfeld sitcom, Mendy's being a restaurant referenced several times, most notably in S7:E6. The Drake is a nickname for a character in S4:E21, bit it might also be a coincidence.
- "The quiz show you answer with a question" that can be seen as a part of QUIZ SHOWS is probably a reference to the Jeopardy! game show.
- One Eighty is a reference to the Bullseye a British darts-themed television game show, having the same concept and core mechanics, with last rounds being all or nothing to win a mystery prize behind the curtain.
- The 'Zoinks!' comic book is a reference to a catchphrase used by Shaggy , one of the main characters of the Scooby-Doo franchise.
- The Cat on a Hot Tin Grill restaurant is a reference to a 1955 play Cat on a Hot Tin Roof .
- Winter Is Coming game mode is a reference to the Game of Thrones , being one of the popular phrases said by the House Stark.
- The Postcard of Mountain Lion Pictures Studio is a reference to the lion mascots used by film studio Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer .
- The book "Just One Last Thing..." is likely a reference to crime-drama TV series Columbo . The author's name "Philip Falk" is referencing Peter Falk , an actor who played Lieutenant Columbo for most of the series.

## The Indie Stone

- T.I.S. Construction , a construction business with multiple sites around Knox County, is a direct reference to The Indie Stone or TIS .
- Spiffo , the game's adorable mascot was a character from PAWS , an unreleased fighting game that Indie Stone were previously working on.
- In several areas of Knox Country, you can find “NASKO” spray-painted on a wall, this is a reference to the Community Manager, nasKo . Germany by Train is written by by Hans N. Asko
- Some areas also have "RingoD" spray-painted on the wall. The graffiti is referencing former modder, turned developer, Paul Ring .
- Enigma Books is a chain of book stores with locations in West Point and Riverside, referencing the Support & Operations Manager, EnigmaGrey .
- The hardware store chain Zac's Hardware in Louisville, refers to developer Zac Congo .
- A cafe in Louisville, Cafe d'Eris is a reference to Eris , a former modder, turned developer.
- The Blue Fox , a restauraunt in Louisville, references developer Fox Chaotica . Blue Fox key ring is also a reference to Fox Chaotica. There are also various posters of hockey players wearing uniform with blue fox logo.
- One of the lunchbox variants has the face of Will Porter surrounded by love hearts. Porter is one of the game's writers. A.A.Ron Hunting Supply is also a reference to Will's son. The business name is also likely a reference to the comedy duo Key & Peele 's substitute teacher sketch. The Thursdoid Compendium is written by Will Porter. Will Power, one of the hosts on KPATV , is also a likely reference to Will Porter. Porter & Son General Store is also a likely reference to Will Porter.
- Mashie Signs & Paintings references Marina Siu-Chong or Mash Potato, main illustrator, artist in TIS. Ashling Dwyer , a fictional painter that was referenced on the Alva Richards (Ashling Dwyer) VHS tape, and on the Art Gallery of Louisville flier was a winner of the 1992 Siu-Chong Art Prize, which is also a reference to Marina Siu-Chong .
- "Drawing Cute Critters" is written by M. Siu-Chong.
- T-shirts can spawn in some rare variants, having references to various The Indie Stone developers A t-shirt with The Indie Stone logo can spawn. A rare variant of the t-shirt has the face of one of the developers, Romain Dron . Another variant can spawn with the bird logo which might be a reference to Aiteron , a modder then hired by The Indie Stone. Another variant can spawn with the wolf logo which might be a reference to Rathlord , former The Indie Stone tech support & server runner.
- R. Johnson Office Building in Louisville, references Robert Johnson/RJ, an alias used by Dron. RJ is also referenced on jars of Remoulade . Romuald Dron Memorial Park was dedicated to the brother of RJ, who has sadly passed away. The park is located in Louisville. Coding, in French is written by R.J. Dron.
- A nightclub in Louisville, Beever's references Zach Beever , one of the composers behind much of the soundtrack.
- Aitor Wire Company , a factory in Louisville, references developer Aitor Garca de la Cruz .

- Blair's Potted Paradise is a gardening store named after one of the developers, Blair Algol.

- The “TEA BOYS” graffiti is a reference to TEA Games , another studio assisting with development.
- Andrei & Son Hand-Crafted Wallets is a factory in Louisville, named after developer Andrei Topilin . Andrei's name is also found as graffiti.
- The clothing store Sasha Sashay in Louisville, is a reference to the lead QA tester, Sasha Mihailova (Pandorea). The brand name on boxing shorts also references Pandorea.
- The graffiti “Yana Yana” is referring to QA tester, Yana

Tech Genius: The Kirrus Story by Emma Gee is an obvious reference to Kirrus.

- There are several references to The Forgotten Element , a point and click game that developers Chris Simpson and Andy Hodgetts had worked on prior to Project Zomboid. ## Community

- Twiggy's is a bar located in West Point , the bar is named after sirtwiggy , a former Project Zomboid YouTuber and streamer. Twiggy is also makes an appearance as one of the callers on KnoxTalk Radio , and a zombie that can be found inside of the bar.
- Stendo's Firearms Emporium is a memorial to Stendo_Clip, who was the founding author of the Vanilla Firearms Expansion mod.
- The " IsAtomic " tag for the Rubber Duck is a reference to youtuber MrAtomicDuck.
- The " Puppers " tag for the Plastic Spork is a reference to a community member Puppers, who asked for its addition. A code name of Glitter ( Clitter ), is also a from Puppers, which started to be an inside joke on Discord .
- KPK Consulting Business Investments in Louisville, references KPKPlays, a friend of TIS.
- Hair O Genesis is a chain of barber shops that have several locations in Knox Country, the chain is named after Ontogenesis, a friend of TIS.
- A car dealership Nolan's Used Cars , located in Dixie Mobile Park , references Nolan Ritchie from undeniable.info , a modder behind several popular mods, one of which being the Driving Cars Mod.
- In the woods outside of Muldraugh, you can find Ben's Cabin , the name is referencing a forum user known as haze1434 and it being their base right before the birth of his child. Haze had to leave to go to the hospital because his wife was about to give birth. Their child was named Ben and the cabin was named after them as a way of commemorating this event.
- The in-game show Moderators , is a reference to an actual event, when someone on the moderation team tried to ban a user from The Indie Stone Forums, but accidentally made them an admin instead. Whoops!
- The advertisement for Incontileve , a fictional brand of bladder control medication, has Dr Cox as the spokesperson for the ad. The doctor's name is a reference to the mod author, Dr_Cox1911.
- Tooks bear and possibly Fryer Tooks Fish N' Chips are named after a friend of The Indie Stone and a former YouTuber and streamer of the same name.
- Ivanov Factory & Showroom is a reference to a community member Leo Ivanov , an author of The Walking Dead Prison mod .
- Blind Coder , Leo Ivanov , Mad Dan, Soul Filcher , Captain Binky and Willie Porter on National Sports TV are named after the various community members and developers.

## Miscellaneous

- The name of the tailoring supplies store chain, Awl Work and Sew Play is a play on the proverb All work and no playmakes Jack a dull boy , known mostly from The Shining movie .
- Mead, Drink and Bloody Merry store name is a play on the phrases such as Wine, women and song or its more known modern counterpart Sex, drugs and rock 'n' roll or the Lock, stock, and barrel , which is most commonly used to mean “everything” and is known from the Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels film .
- Home Sweet Gnome and Homeware Sweet Homeware business names are plays on the idiomatic expression "Home Sweet Home".
- Let There Be Light lamp store is a reference to the most famous line found in the Genesis 1:3, "Let there be light" . That is also commonly used in other media and games.
- There are multiple other bible references, for example church billboard saying "Hungry? Try Forty Days In The Desert! ".
- Better Bed Than Dead business is a play on an anti-communist phrase, "better dead than red" , or its reverse "better red than dead". Red Wine bottle also has a "Red or Dead" on it, potentially alluding to the same phrase.
- Many snacks are parodies of various snacks that were made by Hostess Brands . Be they Plonkies ( Twinkies ), Hi His ( Ho Hos ), Sno Globes ( Sno Balls ), and Choco Cakes ( Hostess CupCakes ). Quagga Cakes is the in-universe equivalent of Zebra Cakes that were produced by McKee Foods. Jujubes is the in-universe equivalent of the similarly named Jujube candy. Happy Curios Cereal is likely a reference to Lucky Charms cereals. Various chocolate bars allude towards most popular brands, like Galactic Dairy ( Milky way , it also uses the U.S. version of the package theming), Deux ( Twix ), Roy's Peanut Butter Pucks ( Reese's Peanut Butter Cups ), Smirkers ( Snickers ), SnikSnak ( Kit Kat ), Crackle ( Crunch ) and Butterchunkers ( Butterfinger ).
- Sometimes a house with containers filled with toilet paper can be found. This is a reference to global supply shortages during the COVID-19 pandemic, with toilet paper being one of the main items suffering shortages due to panic buying.
- A window shop in Louisville named Window is a reference to Microsoft Windows , a series of operating systems developed by Microsoft .
- One of the variants of walkie talkie is called 'Toys-R-Mine Walkie Talkie' The manufacturer's name is a reference to the toy retailer Toys "R" Us . Churns-R-Us may also be a reference to the business, having similarly styled naming.
- The clothing store chain Hugo Plush is a reference to developer Marina Siu-Chong 's former Guinea pig Hugo. Lola Limon is a reference to Lola.
- Blue & White Bar is themed after "KSP" football club, which is a reference to the Queens Park Rangers Football Club , as well as having a writing on the wall, "KSP Shit But Local" which is a reference to the community title of the QPR club.
- Magazine: Secrets of the Outback Outlaws is a reference to 19th-century bushranger Ned Kelly . The magazine is referencing the armour worn by him and his gang during their last stand against police.
- Magazine: FBI Incident Report: San Fernando Bank Robbery is most likely referencing the 1997 San Fernando robbery, better known as the North Hollywood Shootout , notorious for the perpetrators use of improvised heavy armor.
- The Art Gallery of Louisville contains various recreations of real life artwork. A recreation of the 1893 piece The Scream by Norwegian artist Edvard Munch . The 1862 piece Stańczyk by Polish artist Jan Matejko . Several paintings by Dutch artist Vincent van Gogh , such as Sunflowers , one of his self-portraits and his most iconic painting, The Starry Night . Girl with a Pearl Earring by Dutch artist Johannes Vermeer . Wanderer above the Sea of Fog by German artist Caspar David Friedrich . The Son of Man by Belgian artist René Magritte . "Lily's Waterpond Painting" is referencing a 1916 art-piece in a massive series of paintings titled Water Lilies by French artist Claude Monet . "Chromatic Accident" could be referencing American artist Jackson Pollock , famous for his abstract expressionist art pieces. A couple of abstract paintings potentially serving as a homage to Latvian-born American artist Mark Rothko . Saturn Devouring His Son by Spanish artist Francisco Goya . Rokeby Venus by Spanish artist Diego Velázquez . Washington Crossing the Delaware by German-born American artist Emanuel Leutze . The "Riverside Painting" could be referencing the The Oxbow by English-born American artist Thomas Cole . The "Sir Brakefire Painting" could be referencing Portrait of a Bearded Man in Black Beret (1654) by Dutch artist Rembrandt . Venus and Adonis by Flemish artist Peter Paul Rubens . Michelangelo 's Statue of David . The "Female Statue" could be inspired by various statues depicting the biblical figure Rebecca . The Spiffo's burgers are drawn in the style of Andy Warhol's Shot Marilyns . A toilet referencing Fountain by French artist Marcel Duchamp , an infamous art-piece where the sculpture was just a porcelain urinal. A bed referencing another controversial art piece, My Bed by English artist Tracey Emin .
- There's another art gallery next-door that has the "Layout 1", "Configuration 2", "Configuration No. 10" paintings, referencing Dutch artist Piet Mondrian .
- Louisville State University could be referencing American psychologist Carl Rogers .
- Hector Lopez Barbecue seems to be a parody name of the George Foreman Grill . This humorous twist plays on the name of the famous kitchen appliance endorsed by former heavyweight boxing champion George Foreman
