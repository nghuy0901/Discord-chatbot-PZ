# Broadcasts

Entertainment Life and Living TV • Adverts • CD • VHS • Television • Radio

Broadcasts , also called channels or frequencies , are pre-scripted broadcasts that affect moodles , provide the player with some lore , skill experience, or emergency information, such as weather or metagame information. Devices will usually be found with one or more frequencies saved to the presets.

Throughout the first week of the infection, news media and music stations report on the Knox Event , providing players with updates on world events from outside the exclusion zone such as riots, curfews, and the eventual breach of the quarantine.

## Time slots

Each broadcast has an assigned time slot. A player can trigger a broadcast at any point during its time slot, as long as it hasn’t already been played. For example, if a broadcast’s time slot is 6 AM to 12 PM, it will begin automatically at 6 AM if a nearby device is on. If no device is on, it will start as soon as the player comes within range of an active device, even near the end of the slot (e.g., 11:50 AM). If the broadcast finishes during the next time slot, the following broadcast will begin immediately.

This means that when starting a new game, since by default it begins at 9 AM, there are 3 hours left to catch the first broadcast. Players aiming to min-max can tune into Life and Living TV during the 6 AM to 12 PM time slot for a skill boost.

Radio broadcasts are played on radios , walkie talkies and ham radios . Some frequencies are randomized, and must be found on a device before players can tune into them.
