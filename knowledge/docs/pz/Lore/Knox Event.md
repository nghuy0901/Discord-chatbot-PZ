# Knox Event

Lore Businesses • Metagame • Outfit • Survivor • Knox Event • Knox Infection • Raleigh Outbreak • Randomized stories

Spoiler warning! The following contains unhidden spoilers pertaining to the story of Project Zomboid . Proceed at your own risk.

“ This thing, let's call it 'The Knox Event', feels like a powder keg. — Judge Matt Hass

The Knox Event is used to refer to the zombie outbreak that occurs prior to and throughout the game. The event first takes place around Muldraugh and West Point , Kentucky. The incident is considered to have begun on the 4th of July, 1993, which is also known as (American) Independence Day, or the Fourth of July. Just two days later the military evacuated residences from around the affected area and set up blockades in what is now known as the Knox Evacuation . The affected area is designated the Exclusion Zone , with the primary camp being south of Louisville .

It should be noted that current sandbox world does not have a clear day state to it. While it shows signs of post Louisville border collapse, judging by breaches in the fence, as well as looted shops, barricaded buildings, etc. There are also signs of commuters still living their normal lives, like shopping, full of goods stores and crowded places of recreation. It will likely change to show more defined progression of events in the story mode.

## Rumored outbreak causes

- Numerous factors point towards the U.S. Military being responsible for the Knox Event, which include but are not limited to: The military was responsible for several incidents involving public exposure to 'hazardous materials', including a waste spill on July 4th . The military's immediate response to the outbreak and the strictly enforced exclusion zones suggest prior knowledge of the virus and its severity. Much of the information released to the public regarding the Knox Event was relayed by General John McGrew , rather than the President , further suggesting the military taking the lead on the response to the infection. The secluded Military Research Facility near Rosewood , which has facilities for human experimentation and dissection, may have served as the testing grounds for the Knox Virus. It is likely one of the CDC bioweapon labs supposedly hidden throughout the country, as detailed by a former CDC employee on USR . The same employee also appears to have identified that the Knox Infection was a bioweapon known as "Juniper-17," however this is mainly based on assumptions rather than hard evidence. Further observations by a caller on USR claims to have seen personnel in hazmat suits injecting residents of West Point with an unknown serum, supposedly inciting hallucinations in those injected.
- A bad smell had been in the air for weeks before the blockade went up, many believe that's how you get sick, once it gets in your lungs, you're done. 
 "You can smell it in the air. Something's smelled bad for weeks, it gets into your lungs. That's what's behind it." - Christian: Caller on KnoxTalk
- Many people believe something in the Spiffo's meat is the cause of the outbreak; the next evolutionary step for Mad Cow disease. 
 "I've been warning people for years. It's in the meat. We've been feeding our cows on their own kind. Misfolded prions. Brain matter fed back into the food chain. These prions have been building up in countless generations of cattle. This is quite clearly a neurological disorder." - Professor Peter Endsleigh
- Religious folk believe that Man is paying for their sins, and that this is the work of God. 
 "The end is at hand. America has sinned. Every day I look to our streets and I see depravity and wickedness. A nation tainted by perverts and sinners." - Pastor Samuels
- The outbreak occurring so close to Independence Day can't be a coincidence, it must be terrorism. 
 "Terrorism. Terrorism is my point. They've been coming for us for years. This is their moment." - Francine: Caller on KnoxTalk
- Strange dog attacks and dogs coming down with illnesses are reported by numerous sources both before and during the outbreak.
- The strange lights seen in March 1991 might indicate that the infection is extraterrestrial in nature, namely an asteroid.
