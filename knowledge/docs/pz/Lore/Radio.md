# Radio

A radio is an interactive item added in Build 33 . It allows the player to listen to radio broadcasts, including music stations, talk-back stations, news reports, military stations, and adverts . Unlike walkie talkies , radios are receive-only. Most vehicles also include radio equipment, with civilian vehicles having basic radios whilst military or emergency service vehicles can have two-way-capable sets.

## Overview

Radios are one of the three types of communication devices.

**Radio:** Receive only, lightweight, commercial frequencies only.

**Walkie talkie:** Short-range two-way, lightweight, more frequencies.

**Ham radio:** Long-range two-way, exceedingly heavy, most or all frequencies. Earbuds cannot be used. Ham radios are best used as stationary furniture in a safe location.

## Usage

All radios are batteries powered. They will spawn with a battery already installed, however, having the radio turned on will drain its battery until it is consumed. Some radios can be found already turned on, these will have their batteries drained based on how long they have been turned on.

To use a radio, right-click it in the inventory or the world and select Device Options . This will open the radio control UI. Here you can see information about the radio's specifications and current settings, power it on or off, remove/replace the battery, adjust the volume, add earbuds or headphones , and select and edit preset frequencies.

Two-way radios, such as the ham radio , will transmit and reproduce voices at a distance, while the standard radio can only receive and not transmit. The reproduction will be silent to nearby players and zombies if the receiving radio uses earbuds; otherwise, it will make noise in the environment depending on the volume setting. Bear in mind that only one radio in a given area will reproduce the voice, and if the person speaking is nearby, it will not reproduce it at all to prevent feedback. Radios farther away (generally beyond visual range) will still pick up and reproduce the voices.

### CDs

The Premium Technologies Radio has the ability to also play CDs , which works in a similar fashion to the CD Player .

### AEBS

Radios can also listen to the Automated Emergency Broadcast System (AEBS) radio station, which is randomized every game. Once the player finds a radio with the AEBS present saved, they can enter the frequency for it on any other radio and thus unlock it. By listening to this radio station, the player can get hints about the oncoming weather, and possible helicopter event and power shutoff events.

### Helpful Information with Frequenices

- Random MHz frequency stations require the player to add a preset with the correct radio spectrum manually. The player can find available presets of these stations on pre-placed radios throughout the world.
- Throughout the first week of the infection, news media and music stations will report on the Knox Event , and give the player outside information on world events. These events include riots, curfews being put into effect, and eventually, the breaking of the quarantine.
- Both scripted and non-scripted radio broadcasts will still occur after the media blackout.
- The Automated Emergency Broadcast System (AEBS) will have a random frequency between 88 MHz and 108 MHz that can be divided nicely by 0.2 MHz. Example frequencies: 88 MHz, 92.6 MHz, 103.4 MHz. 108 MHz is not a possible value for the AEBS. As it cannot be an already taken frequency, there are 95 possible frequencies for AEBS.
